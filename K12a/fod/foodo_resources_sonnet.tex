\section{Introduction}
Foodo (Guang; ISO 639-3 \texttt{fod}) is a Guang language spoken mainly around S\`em\`er\`e in northern Benin, a ``linguistic island''. It is written in the Latin alphabet, though few speakers are literate in it. Many Foodo speak Tem as a trade language. Published research is limited.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Foodo has national-language status in Benin and a Latin-script writing system, but little literacy and no school materials were found. A curriculum would need to begin with orthography consolidation and primer development, using the Plunkett grammar sketch as a linguistic base.
