\section{Introduction}
Tamil (ISO 639-3 tam; Glottocode tami1289) is a Dravidian language and an official language in parts of India, used as a language of instruction. The language is very well resourced. This report is a short starting list from limited searching, not a complete survey.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Tamil Nadu School Education Department e-books page (Classes 1--12, Tamil medium available), reported by guides as \url{https://tnschools.gov.in/scert}; I did not see this page itself in search results, only guides citing it. Third-party mirror sites also appeared and are not recommended.
\item Samacheer Kalvi textbook background: \url{https://patrimoine.minesparis.psl.eu/Wiki/index.php/Samacheer_Kalvi_Wikipedia}
\end{itemize}
