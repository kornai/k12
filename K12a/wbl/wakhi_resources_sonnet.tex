\section{Introduction}
Wakhi (ISO 639-3 wbl) is spoken in Afghanistan, Pakistan and Tajikistan. Literacy materials exist in Afghanistan and Pakistan, in different scripts.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item The zabanha.af page for Wakhi lists a Wakhi Literacy Primer, a Transfer Primer for readers of Dari, an Alphabet Book, a Preschool Primer, teacher's guides for some, a Wakhi keyboard file and a collection of proverbs in Wakhi and Dari: \url{https://zabanha.af/languages/wakhi}
\item Related zabanha.af page: \url{https://zabanha.af/node/11}
\item Pamir Times review of a sociolinguistic study of Wakhi in northern Pakistan, which reports annexes containing the first Roman-script ``Wakhi Primer'' by Haqiqat Ali and a Perso-Arabic/Urdu-script booklet: \url{https://pamirtimes.net/2014/10/21/book-review-a-sociolinguistic-study-of-wakhi-language-of-northern-pakistan/}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Competing scripts (Perso-Arabic in Afghanistan, Roman and Urdu-based in Pakistan) would need to be reconciled or chosen per region. Primers and teacher's guides exist for the Afghan side and could serve as a base.
