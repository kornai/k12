\section{Introduction}
Nobiin (ISO 639-3 fia; also Halfawi, Mahas) is a Northern Nubian language of the Nilo-Saharan family, spoken in Sudan and Egypt, with two main dialects (Mahas and Fadija). Speaker estimates vary widely, from roughly 500,000 to 900,000. There is no standardised orthography: Latin, Arabic and a revived Old Nubian-based script are all in use.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Nobiin is widely spoken but not taught in schools as far as found. Orthography is unsettled, and materials are community-led and diaspora-based. A curriculum would need an orthography decision first.
