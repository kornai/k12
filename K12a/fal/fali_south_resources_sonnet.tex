\section{Introduction}
South Fali (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fal}) is spoken in northern Cameroon by about 20,000 people; listed varieties include Kaang (Fali Kangou), Bele (Fali-Bele) and Fali-Tinguelin. The Peske variety belongs to North Fali, not South Fali. Search found no dictionary, primer or school programme.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A stable community of roughly 20,000 speakers is a reasonable base, but no educational materials were found; development would require new orthography, primer and reading material work. The ethnographic overview at \url{https://www.everyculture.com/Africa-Middle-East/Fali-Orientation.html} gives cultural background.
