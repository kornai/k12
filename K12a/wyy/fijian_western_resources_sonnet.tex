\section{Introduction}
Western Fijian (ISO 639-3: wyy) is an Oceanic language of Fiji, distinct from Eastern (Bauan) Fijian, the standard. It covers several dialects, including Nadroga, Ba and Wayan. Published resources are mostly for the Wayan dialect, and I found no school curriculum materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes specific to Western Fijian were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Western Fijian is spoken by tens of thousands of people, but the published materials are linguistic and cover mainly Waya and the Yasawa dialects. I found no Ba or Nadi dictionary or grammar. A curriculum would need to choose a dialect, probably Nadroga or Wayan, and write teaching materials from these sources. Fijian schooling generally uses the standard Eastern variety.
