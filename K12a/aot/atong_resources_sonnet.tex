\section{Introduction}
Atong (natively A$\cdot$tong; ISO 639-3 aot) is a Sino-Tibetan (Tibeto-Burman) language spoken in the Garo Hills and Khasi Hills of Meghalaya, India, and across the border in Netrokona District, Bangladesh. It is closely related to Garo, Koch, Rabha and Bodo and is classed as severely endangered. Search results turned up scholarly documentation but no school materials.
