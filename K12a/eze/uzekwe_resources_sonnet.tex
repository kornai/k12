\section{Introduction}
Uzekwe (Zekwe; ISO 639-3 \texttt{eze}) is an Upper Cross River language of Cross River State, Nigeria, per the search results. Joshua Project lists a population of about 19,000 and records Scripture status as ``Translation Started'' and the language as not written. Very little else was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language appears essentially undocumented for educational purposes; no orthography was found. Curriculum work would begin with orthography development and community consultation. The ``Ezekwe'' pages found concern Igbo families and are unrelated.
