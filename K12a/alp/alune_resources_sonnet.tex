\section{Introduction}
Alune (ISO 639-3 alp) is an Austronesian language of west Seram, Maluku, Indonesia. It is not known to be taught in schools. Web search found little that is directly usable as classroom material; this report lists only what appeared in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No Alune primer, textbook or school programme was found in search results. A 2004 SIL illustrated Alune--Indonesian picture dictionary is mentioned in a journal article (see below), but no URL for the dictionary itself was seen.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Wikipedia, Alune language (overview and bibliography, including \emph{Struktur Bahasa Alune}, 1997): \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alune_language}
\item \emph{Genta Bahtera} article on the phoneme inventory of the West Central Alune dialect, mentioning the 2015 dictionary project and the SIL picture dictionary: \url{https://gentabahtera.kemdikbud.go.id/index.php/gentabahtera/article/download/82/56}
\item Verbix, short Alune verb list: \url{https://docs.verbix.com/Languages/Alune}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Alune Bible (ALPWBT) at Digital Bible Society: \url{https://dbs.org/bibles/ALPWBT}
\end{itemize}

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Glosbe crowd-sourced Alune translation pages (word lists, not a scholarly dictionary): \url{https://glosbe.com/alp/lib}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Materials are scarce. A curriculum would need to build on the phonological descriptions, the Bible translation text and any dictionary held by the Maluku language office, which should be contacted directly. Orthography is Latin script.
