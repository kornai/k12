\section{Introduction}
Alune (ISO 639-3 \texttt{alp}) is an Austronesian language of western Seram, Maluku, Indonesia. Search results gave speaker estimates well below the 100,000 in the survey identification (one source: about 17,000) and list several dialects (Central East, Central West, Kairatu, North Coastal, South Alune). Little published material exists, and none found is a school curriculum.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No Alune primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in search results. Search results mention an SIL illustrated Alune--Indonesian picture dictionary (2004) but no URL was found, so it is not listed as a resource here.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Audio accompanying the New Testament is reported in search results (via Faith Comes By Hearing); no direct URL was seen, so none is cited. No other audio, video or corpus was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Alune is not known to be taught in schools, and no comprehensive modern grammar or digitized full dictionary was found. A 2015 dictionary compilation project by the Maluku language office was mentioned but not verified. A curriculum would need to start from the 1997 grammar, the Bible translation text and new community-led material.
