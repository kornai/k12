\section{Introduction}
Faroese (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fao}, Glottocode faro1244) is the national language of the Faroe Islands and the language of instruction in Faroese schools. Search results indicate a functioning native-language school system, a dedicated educational publisher (N\'am), a national dictionary portal, and a Bible translation. The main gap reported is materials for Faroese as a second language.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\item \emph{Finka -- f{\o}royskt til annan flokk}, a second-grade Faroese textbook by Marianna Skar\dh enni Poulsen; it won gold at the 2020 Best European Learning Materials Awards. \url{https://kvf.fo/greinar/2020/10/14/faroese-schoolbook-wins-major-european-award}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Faroese already has a native-language school system and textbook publisher, so a curriculum can build on existing N\'am materials and the Sprotin and spelling dictionaries. A 2024 study reported in the search results found a severe shortage of materials for Faroese as a second language, which is the clearest gap.
