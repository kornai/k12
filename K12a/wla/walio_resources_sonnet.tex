\section{Introduction}
Walio (ISO 639-3 wla; Glottocode nucl1453) is spoken in Walio village, East Sepik Province, Papua New Guinea. Wikipedia gives about 230 speakers (2003), and Joshua Project gives about 400, citing Ethnologue 2016. Classification varies: Wikipedia treats it as Sepik, while Glottolog treats the Walio languages (with Pei, Yawiyo and Tuwari) as a separate family. This report is based on a single round of web searches, and I opened none of the linked pages.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The search results show no reported writing system and no published teaching materials. Any curriculum would begin with documentation and orthography work with the Walio community. The materials found are reference pages only and cannot support a K-12 curriculum on their own.
