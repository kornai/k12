\section{Introduction}
Kaqchikel is a Mayan language of Guatemala. The searches did not separate the Northern variety (ckc) from Kaqchikel generally, so the items below concern Kaqchikel as a whole and may not match Northern Kaqchikel specifically. (The family label given in the prompt, ``Bookkeeping'', appears to be a data error; Kaqchikel is Mayan.)

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
Press reports from the Guatemalan news agency say the Ministry of Education distributed bilingual textbooks in eight Mayan languages including Kaqchikel and publishes them as PDFs on its portal. I did not find a specific Kaqchikel title list, so none is cited.
