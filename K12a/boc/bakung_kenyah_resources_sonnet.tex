\section{Introduction}
Bakung Kenyah (ISO 639-3 boc; Glottocode baku1265) is a Kenyah variety of Borneo. The family field in the request (``Bookkeeping'') appears to be a data error; the Kenyah languages are Austronesian (Malayo-Polynesian). Search shows ``Bakung'' used as an alternate name or dialect label within Mainstream Kenyah (xkl), a cluster of about 50,000 speakers in North Kalimantan (Bulungan and Malinau) and northern Sarawak (Usun Apau, Telang Usan, Belaga). Sources disagree on exact dialect membership. No Bakung-specific resources were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Because Bakung is treated as part of the wider Kenyah cluster, any curriculum would depend on local documentation and on the Indonesian (North Kalimantan) or Malaysian (Sarawak) school context. Community-based documentation would be required.
