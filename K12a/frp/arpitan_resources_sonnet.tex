\section{Introduction}
Arpitan (Franco-Proven\c{c}al) is spoken in eastern France, Romandy and north-western Italy. It is classed as severely endangered by UNESCO. In France it has never been an optional school language. Search results were thin and mostly general.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Acad\'emie de Grenoble regional-languages discovery week document, with a La Fontaine fable in Dauphin\'e Franco-Proven\c{c}al and a song with texts: \url{https://savoie-educ.web.ac-grenoble.fr/sites/default/files/Media/document/semaine_langues_langues-regionales.pdf}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
There is no established school curriculum. Teaching depends on local initiatives; the Grenoble materials are a starting point. Dialect and spelling variation is large.
