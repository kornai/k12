\section{Introduction}
Piedmontese (piemont\`eis, ISO 639-3 pms) is a Romance language of north-western Italy with a long written tradition. This report is based on a small number of search results and is not exhaustive.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Duberti and Tosco (2021), ``Teaching Piedmontese'', an academic account of school teaching of Piedmontese, including the regional ARBUT programme (classes from 2000; regional funding ended 2011, per the search summary): \url{https://iris.unito.it/retrieve/339cf423-8037-4ec6-b693-84b36791ca03/DUBERTI%20%26%20TOSCO_2021_Teaching%20Piedmontese.pdf}
\item Bertola, ``Introduction to Piemonteis'', a short online introduction to reading, writing and pronunciation: \url{https://bertola.eu/piemonteis/intro/?_l=en&_1=1}
\end{itemize}
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Wikipedia article on Piedmontese (history of orthography, early grammar by Pipino 1783, the 1930s \emph{grafia piemontese moderna}): \url{https://EN.wikipedia.com/wiki/Piedmontese}
\item Bertola, ``Piemonteis: How to continue'', pointers to further learning sites: \url{https://bertola.eu/piemonteis/intro/end.php?_l=en&_1=1}
\end{itemize}

None identified in the search results.
