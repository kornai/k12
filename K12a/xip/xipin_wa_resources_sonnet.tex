\section{Introduction}
Xipin\'awa (ISO 639-3 xip; Glottocode xipi1238) is an unattested language of South America. Glottolog's entry is the only direct source I found. It lists the language as unattested within Pano-Tacanan. No wordlist, grammar, or description turned up. I found nothing linking Xipin\'awa to a specific community or giving a speaker count.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None exist as far as I could find.

None found.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
None found for Xipin\'awa. The AILLA collection above covers related Panoan languages, not this one.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Because the language is unattested, no curriculum in or about Xipin\'awa can currently be built. The honest outcome is a documentation gap. Neighbouring Panoan languages are described, but I did not verify that any of them is closely related to Xipin\'awa.
