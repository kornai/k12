\section{Introduction}
Kalispel-Pend d'Oreille (ISO 639-3 fla; Glottocode kali1308) is a Salishan language of the northwestern United States, critically endangered. This survey is based on a small number of web searches and lists only resources seen in results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Kalispel Tribe, Salish Curriculum page (Kalispel I and Kalispel Stories I for junior high and high school students at Cusick, plus adult classes; the page is undated): \url{https://kalispeltribe.com/our-language/curriculum}
\end{itemize}

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The tribe's multimedia Salish-to-English online dictionary (open access, with a mobile app according to search summaries) is described on the Kalispel Tribe pages above.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The tribe runs a language programme with school and adult classes and has a dictionary and curriculum in development. No complete K-12 curriculum was found in these searches.
