\section{Introduction}
Central Bikol (ISO 639-3 bcl; Glottocode cent2087) is an Austronesian language of the Bicol region of Luzon, Philippines. Its standard written form is based on the Canaman dialect and is used in literature, Catholic rites and mass media. Under the Philippine K-12 programme (Republic Act 10533), Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education requires the mother tongue as medium of instruction from Kindergarten to Grade 3. Search results suggested that Bikol Naga was chosen as the regional L1 although the provinces differ in dialect.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Central Bikol is a language of instruction under MTB-MLE for the early grades. Gaps found: no current classroom-oriented grammar or dictionary was surfaced, and materials beyond Grade 3 appear scarce. Dialect variation across the six provinces is a design issue.
