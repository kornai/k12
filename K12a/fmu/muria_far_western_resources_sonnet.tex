\section{Introduction}
Far Western Muria (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fmu}; Glottocode farw1235; also Gaita Koitor) is a Dravidian language of India. Sources treat classification and speaker numbers inconsistently, and ``Muria'' and ``Bastar Gondi'' labels overlap, so care is needed not to confuse it with other Muria varieties (e.g., Western Muria). Very little educational material was found.
