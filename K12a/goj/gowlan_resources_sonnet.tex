\section{Introduction}
Gowlan (ISO 639-3 goj) is listed as a small Indo-Aryan language of India, placed by Glottolog in Amravati District, Maharashtra. Glottolog doubts that it is a distinct language: the names Gowari, Gaoli, Gowli and Gowlan appear to refer to one caste, and the goj and gok entries may be spurious. The reference description given with this task (family ``Bookkeeping'', Indo-European) should be treated with caution. Very little material exists, and none found is suitable for a school curriculum.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Before any curriculum work, the identity of the language and its community must be established by fieldwork or consultation with the community; the available evidence suggests the speakers may use a regional Indo-Aryan variety (e.g.\ Marathi or Hindi) in school.
