\section{Introduction}
Humla (hut), also called Humla Tibetan, is a Tibetic language of Humla district, Nepal, with about 5,000 speakers. Speakers name it only generally or by village dialect. Searches found one sociolinguistic survey and background pages; no dictionary, grammar, or school materials were found.
