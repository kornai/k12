\section{Introduction}
The code fas is the ISO 639-3 macrolanguage code for Persian (Farsi). My search found only materials for learners of Persian as a foreign language, not Iran's national school textbooks. This is a thin report and should be extended by a search of the Iranian Ministry of Education textbook portal.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No school (K-12) textbooks were found in search results.
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
University-level textbooks: \emph{Modern Persian, Elementary Level} (Anthem Press), \url{https://anthempress.com/modern-persian-elementary-level-pb-33937}; \emph{Modern Persian: Written and Spoken, Intermediate Texts} (Talatoff, CERCLL), \url{https://cercll.arizona.edu/textbook}.
\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found.
\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The Anthem textbook has a companion website per the publisher listing; no separate URL was seen.
\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Persian is the national and school language of Iran, so mother-tongue curriculum materials surely exist, but they were not found in this search. The items above serve only as foreign-language pedagogy references.
