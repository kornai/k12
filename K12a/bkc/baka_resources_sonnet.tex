\section{Introduction}
Baka (ISO 639-3 bkc) is an Ubangian language of the Atlantic-Congo family spoken by Baka forest people in Cameroon and Gabon. Search results describe it as a dialect cluster, note that about 30\% of its vocabulary is not Ubangian (largely forest-economy terms), and state that it has no standard orthography. No school materials were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Baka were found in the search results.

\item Endangered Languages Project bibliography for Baka, listing among others Paulin's work on Gabonese Baka, L\'eonard's \textit{Baka Oral Narratives} (SIL Cameroon, 2011) and Djoup\'ee's \textit{Morphologie Nominale du Baka} (2002): \url{https://www.endangeredlanguages.com/lang/5989/bibliography}
\end{itemize}
No Baka dictionary was confirmed; the Glosbe pages returned were generic and not trustworthy.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Baka is described as widely spoken in its community, but there is no standard orthography and no known school materials. An orthography decision and community consultation would be a first step; the grammar and SIL narratives above are possible starting points.
