\section{Introduction}
Kanjari (ISO 639-3 kft) is an Indo-Aryan language associated with the Kanjar people; about 210,000 speakers are reported (2011). Sources disagree on its geography and classification. Very little material was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found.
