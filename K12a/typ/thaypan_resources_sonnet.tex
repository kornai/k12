\section{Introduction}
Thaypan (Kuku-Thaypan, also Awu Laya or Gugu Dhayban; ISO 639-3 typ, Glottocode thay1248) is a Paman language of the Pama-Nyungan family, spoken on the southwestern Cape York Peninsula in Queensland. Search results report that the last native speaker, Tommy George, died in 2016, so no first-language speakers remain. This report is short because the searches found little that is usable in a classroom.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
The searches found no primer, textbook or school programme for Thaypan.

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
The searches found none.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Endangered Languages Archive (ELAR) text samples. These come from a collaborative documentation project on five highly endangered Paman languages of Cape York that includes Kuku Thaypan: \url{https://endangeredlanguages.com/lang/3446/samples/11510}
\end{itemize}
The ASJP page the search returned (Thayore) is for a different language and is not listed here.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is dormant, so any curriculum would be a heritage and revitalization effort built from archival material. The ELAR texts and the AIATSIS records are the places to start. I did not check what the ELAR collection contains or who may access it. Any use needs the consent of the community and the descendants of the speakers.
