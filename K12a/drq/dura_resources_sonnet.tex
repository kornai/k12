\section{Introduction}
Dura (ISO 639-3 \texttt{drq}; Glottocode dura1244) is a Sino-Tibetan language formerly spoken in Lamjung District, Nepal, called Dur\=a Bh\~u by the community. It is dormant or extinct; speakers have shifted to Nepali. Census counts of ethnic Dura cited on Wikipedia vary (for example 2,160 in 2011 and other figures), and these are not speaker counts.
