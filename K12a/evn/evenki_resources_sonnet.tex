\section{Introduction}
Evenki (ISO 639-3 \texttt{evn}, Glottocode even1259) is an endangered Tungusic language spoken in Russia, China and Mongolia. Searches found grammars, orthography descriptions and general references, but no current school textbook listings.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
Search results mention Soviet-era readers (e.g. a first reader on the Tungus language compiled by Vasilevich, Latin script from 1931, Cyrillic from 1937) and school teaching reportedly since 1980, but no specific current textbook with a URL was found.
