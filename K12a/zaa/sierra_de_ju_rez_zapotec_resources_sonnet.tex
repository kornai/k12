\section{Introduction}
Sierra de Ju\'arez Zapotec (ISO 639-3 zaa; Glottocode sier1250) is an Otomanguean language of Oaxaca, Mexico, in the Zapotec macrolanguage. The reference description gives about 150 speakers, though Glottolog lists the variety as threatened. Varieties named in sources include Atepec and Macuiltianguis. Little material specific to this language turned up in my searches, and some of it concerns neighbouring Sierra Zapotec varieties.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
My searches found no current school textbooks. Older Summer Institute of Linguistics (ILV) primers and readers for Atepec exist in the Colegio de M\'exico repository, but I did not confirm which record is which, so I do not cite them individually. The repository is at \url{https://repositorio.colmex.mx/concern/books/3t945v18q?locale=en}.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The speaker community is small and the documentation is thin. Curriculum work would rest on archival ILV materials and the multi-variety dictionary, plus new documentation with speakers. The community and orthography decisions for the specific variety need local confirmation.
