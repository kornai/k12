\section{Introduction}
Taupota (ISO 639-3 tpa) is an Austronesian (Oceanic) language of Milne Bay Province, Papua New Guinea. Sources describe it as a dialect chain, with southern Wa'ema and western Wedau varieties. The Ethnologue figure of about 4,500 speakers dates from 2000--2001, while Joshua Project lists 2,600 for the Taupota (Maivara) people. Very little material was found in search, and what follows is short.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Taupota is not known to be taught in schools, and no educational materials were found. Wedau, which Anglican missionaries used as a lingua franca on the southern coast of Goodenough Bay, and Police Motu are the likely regional contact languages. Any curriculum would need new materials developed with the community. This dossier is short because little was found.
