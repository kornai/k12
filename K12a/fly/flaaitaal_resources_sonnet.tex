\section{Introduction}
Flaaitaal (also Tsotsitaal, Fly Taal, Isicamtho) is a South African urban speech variety, originally Afrikaans-based in structure with vocabulary from Setswana, isiXhosa, isiZulu and other languages. Recent scholarship characterises it as a register or style rather than a language with an ethnic community. Some sources describe it as moribund in the townships, with older speakers. This dossier is short because few educational materials were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Flaaitaal has no ethnic community and is not known to be taught. A curriculum would most plausibly be a sociolinguistic awareness unit within Afrikaans or other South African language classes, drawing on the academic literature above.
