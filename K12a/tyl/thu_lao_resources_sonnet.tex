\section{Introduction}
Thu Lao (ISO 639-3 tyl; autonym La H\"u, ``black earth'') is a Tai language spoken in villages of Muong Khuong and Si Ma Cai districts, Lao Cai Province, Vietnam. Glottolog treats the tyl entry as the Vietnamese-side form of Dai Zhuang (zhd), spoken mainly in Yunnan, China, and lists it as retired. The ``Bookkeeping'' family label in the task description is a Glottolog placeholder, not a real family. Joshua Project gives a population of about 200 for Thu Lao in Vietnam. Little material exists.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Thu Lao is not known to be taught. A 2025 report says Vietnam plans to introduce the Lao language in border-area schools, but that concerns standard Lao and not Thu Lao: \url{https://www.tourismlaos.org/2025/06/16/vietnam-to-introduce-lao-language-in-general-education-near-border-areas/}.
