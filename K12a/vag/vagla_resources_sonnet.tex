\section{Introduction}
Vagla (ISO 639-3: vag) is a Gur language of Ghana's Savannah Region with about 14,000 speakers. It is written in the Latin alphabet, with doubled letters for long vowels.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Vagla is not known to be taught in schools. The GILLBT dictionary and Bible translation provide a basis for orthography-based reading materials; details should be confirmed with GILLBT.
