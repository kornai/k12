\section{Introduction}
Beeke (Beke, Ibeeke; ISO 639-3 \texttt{bkf}) is a Bantu language of the Democratic Republic of the Congo with fewer than about 1,000 speakers, reportedly giving way to Ndaka. Its classification is uncertain (possibly a divergent form of Bali). Only descriptive overviews were found.
