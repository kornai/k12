\section{Introduction}
Beeke (ISO 639-3 bkf) is a Bantu (Atlantic-Congo) language of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, spoken in Ituri along the Mambasa--Kisangani road. Its classification is uncertain; it may be a divergent form of Bali. Estimates put speakers at under about 1,000, and Endangered Languages notes it is giving way to Ndaka. Joshua Project reports no Bible translation or Jesus Film for it. No educational materials were found.
