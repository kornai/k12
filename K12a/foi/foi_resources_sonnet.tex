\section{Introduction}
Foi (ISO 639-3 \texttt{foi}; Glottocode foii1241), also written Foe or Mubi River, is one of the two East Kutubuan languages of the Trans-New Guinea family, spoken along Lake Kutubu and the Mubi River in Papua New Guinea. Wikipedia gives 6,000 speakers (2015); Joshua Project gives about 9,800 Foi people.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Foi is not known to be taught in schools. The existing printed materials are a New Testament and an older ethnography/language book, so a curriculum would depend on new community-led materials; the 1978 New Testament shows an established written form.
