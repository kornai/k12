\section{Introduction}
Setswana (ISO 639-3 tsn, Glottocode tswa1253) is an official language of Botswana and South Africa and is used as a language of instruction. This report rests on one search, which mainly returned South African CAPS materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\item Grade 6 Learner's Book: \url{https://viaafrika.com/?p=15760}
\item Grade 7 Learner's Book: \url{https://viaafrika.com/?p=10214}

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Only the ePDF learner's books above were found. No audio, corpus or software.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
South Africa has a commercial CAPS-aligned Setswana Home Language series for Grades 5--11 and free DBE guides. Botswana materials, early grades (R--4) and Grade 12 were not found in this search and need further work.
