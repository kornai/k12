\section{Introduction}
Wagi (ISO 639-3: fad; Glottocode wagi1249) is a Trans-New Guinea language of Madang Province, Papua New Guinea, also recorded under the names Kamba, Kauris, Silibob and Mis Furan. Sources give about 3{,}400 speakers (2003) and rate it as vigorous. Searches found only a small amount of material; no school primers, textbooks, or dictionaries were found, and none are claimed here.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school materials in Wagi were found in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Wagi is used by all adults and many but not all young people, so a mother-tongue curriculum would have to be built largely from scratch, starting from an orthography and the SIL survey findings on dialect variation. No existing school programme was found.
