\section{Introduction}
Fagani (ISO 639-3: faf; Glottocode faga1239) is an Austronesian (San Cristobal) language of northwest Makira, Solomon Islands. Wikipedia gives about 900 speakers (a 1999 figure) and lists the dialects Agufi, Fagani and Rihu'a; UNESCO classifies it as vulnerable. Search turned up very little beyond general reference pages.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
There is no known school use of Fagani. Any curriculum would need to begin with locating the Capell/Paradisec holdings and any Bible portions, and with community consultation on orthography and speaker numbers, since the available figures are dated.
