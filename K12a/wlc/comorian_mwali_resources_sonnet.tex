\section{Introduction}
Mwali Comorian (Shimwali, Mohélien; ISO 639-3 wlc; Glottocode mwal1237) is spoken on Mwali (Mohéli) in the Comoros. It is one of the four Comorian varieties. The western group consists of Shimwali and Shingazija. This report is based on a single round of web searches. I found no standalone Mwali dictionary or grammar, and I opened none of the linked pages.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
I found no primers or textbooks specific to Mwali.

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
Scripture Earth lists scripture resources for wlc, including a link to Kalaam Media, a website for the Mwali language: \url{https://www.scriptureearth.org/00eng.php?iso=wlc} and \url{https://www.scriptureearth.org/00i-Scripture_Index.php?iso=wlc}. I did not open the Kalaam Media site and saw no direct URL for it, so I cannot describe what it holds.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The Dictionnaire ORELC iOS app is a digital dictionary covering Comorian varieties including shiMwali (Mohélien), with lookups to French and other languages: \url{https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6474321239}. It is a multi-variety tool, not a Mwali-only dictionary.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The reference description says the language is not known to be taught in schools. Little Mwali-specific material turned up, so a curriculum would likely have to draw on general Comorian materials and on community and Comorian-institution partners. The cross-variety resources above would need checking for Mwali coverage.
