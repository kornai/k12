\section{Introduction}
Seychellois Creole (Kreol Seselwa, ISO 639-3 crs) is an official national language of Seychelles and is used in education, notably as medium of instruction in the early school years. The standardizing body is Lenstiti Kreol, and the Seychelles Creole Academy has recently published a monolingual dictionary. The searches found no specific school textbook.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No named textbook was found. One source states Kreol is the medium of instruction from pre-school to primary 2 (ages 3--7); see the Wikivoyage and news pages below for context.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language already has a standard orthography, codified grammar and dictionaries, including a trilingual Kreol--French--English dictionary (2017, Collette Gillieaux) and an earlier de St Jorre and Lionnet dictionary, both reported in the news items above. Curriculum work should contact Lenstiti Kreol and the Ministry of Education for current school materials.
