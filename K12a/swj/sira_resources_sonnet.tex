\section{Introduction}
Sira (also Shira or Eshira, ISO 639-3 swj, Guthrie B.41) is a Bantu language of Gabon with about 39,000 speakers (a 2000 figure). Very little was found: no dictionary, textbook, or school material appeared in the search results. Only reference pages and linguistic articles were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}

The Royal Museum for Central Africa pottery vocabulary database lists a single Sira term: \url{https://www.africamuseum.be/en/research/discover/human_sciences/culture_society/cb-pottery/comparative/3599/vocabulary}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Sira is not known to be taught in schools. Documentation is thin, limited to a few linguistic articles on tone and short wordlists. A curriculum would first need basic documentation such as a dictionary, orthography and grammar. French is the likely language of schooling in Gabon, but the searches did not address this.
