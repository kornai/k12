\section{Introduction}
This report covers Tz'utujil (ISO 639-3 tzt, Glottocode tzut1249). My searches returned general Tz'utujil resources and nothing specific to the Western variety. Dialect coverage of each item should be checked before use.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
I found no textbooks or school programmes in the search results. The Wikipedia entry on the language mentions the community library Rija' Tzuul Na'ooj in San Juan La Laguna, which offers children's storytelling in Tz'utujil and bilingual children's books. I did not see a library page, so I list no URL for it.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Vocabulario Tz'utujil, a 2025 reprint published by the Academia de Lenguas Mayas de Guatemala (ALMG). \url{https://almg.org.gt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/VOCABULARIO-TZUTUJIL-VERSION-FINAL-2025.pdf}
\item Glottolog lists these older works: Dayley, \emph{Tzutujil Grammar} (1981 and 1985); P\'erez Mendoza and Hern\'andez Mendoza, \emph{Diccionario Tz'utujil} (1996); and Garc\'ia Ixmat\'a, \emph{Rukeemiik ja tz'utujiil chii'} (1997). \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/iso/tzj}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
I found none in the search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Tz'utujil Talking Dictionary with audio recordings, from the Living Tongues Institute. \url{https://talkingdictionaries.app/tzutujil}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Reference works exist: the ALMG vocabulary, the talking dictionary and older grammars. The search results showed no complete curriculum, readers or textbooks. The variety coverage of every item should be checked with the ALMG or the Living Tongues project.
