\section{Introduction}
Silesian (ISO 639-3 szl) is spoken in Silesia, in south-western Poland. Search results show a standardization effort: a 2008 conference in Katowice and a 2009 working group produced a standard spelling, according to a 2014 review. They also mention a Silesian-language primer, but the excerpt did not name it. I did not find a formal school textbook or an official curriculum.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item A free online beginner course derived from Wikibooks. It is aimed at non-Polish speakers, uses the \emph{\'slabik\^orzowy szrajb\={o}nek} spelling, and includes a Silesian--English dictionary. The URL is a mirror copy: \url{https://kwx.liehendr.com/content/wikibooks_en_all_maxi_2021-03/A/Silesian}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{S\l ownik Gwar \'Sl\k askich} (Dictionary of Silesian Dialects), a multi-volume scholarly work from the Silesian Institute in Opole. Volume I (A--BECZKA) is listed at \url{https://ceeol.com/search/book-detail?id=531599}
\item Other volumes found: volume XIII (HYRO--JU\.ZYNECZKI), \url{https://ceeol.com/search/book-detail?id=532470}; volume II, \url{https://ceeol.com/search/book-detail?id=532353}
\item Bogdan Kallus, \emph{S\l ownik g\`orno\'sl\`onskij godki} (2007), a shortened Upper Silesian--Polish dictionary that also proposes a spelling. I saw no URL for it, so it is not in the JSON list.
\item Cambridge \emph{Nationalities Papers} article on Silesian in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries: \url{https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/nationalities-papers/article/silesian-in-the-nineteenth-and-twentieth-centuries-a-language-caught-in-the-net-of-conflicting-nationalisms-politics-and-identities/F0B364407B367D4FCBF0A792CB81ECB6}
\item Hokkaido University repository PDF (ASI35\_008), on Silesian: \url{https://eprints.lib.hokudai.ac.jp/repo/huscap/all/60021/ASI35_008.pdf}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None identified in these search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Apart from the online course above, I found no audio, video or corpus resources in these searches.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools, and no ready-made classroom materials turned up. Silesian cultural organizations and the Silesian Institute should be asked directly. Spelling varies, so a curriculum needs to choose one convention, such as the one in the Wikibooks course.
