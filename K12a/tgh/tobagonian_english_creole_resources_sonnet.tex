\section{Introduction}
Tobagonian English Creole (ISO 639-3 tgh) is an English-based creole of Tobago. The searches found no Tobago-specific teaching materials, grammar or corpus. The only substantial resource found is a dictionary that covers the English/Creole of Trinidad and Tobago together. Little else turned up, and the sections below say so.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Tobagonian Creole turned up in the searches.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Lise Winer (ed.), \emph{Dictionary of the English/Creole of Trinidad \& Tobago: On Historical Principles} (McGill-Queen's University Press, 2009). It covers both islands, not Tobago alone, and describes over 12,200 entries. Catalogue record: \url{https://unicat.nalis.bg/Record/AUBG.000460762/Description}
\item Review and description of the dictionary: \url{https://kreolmagazine.com/dictionary-of-the-english-creole-of-trinidad-tobago-on-historical-principles/amp/}
\item Library record: \url{https://find.mtsu.edu/vufind/Record/mig00005378412}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found in the searches.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The search results mention Winer's research papers at the University of the West Indies archive. They are likely to contain sound recordings and research material, but I did not verify their contents: \url{https://archivespace.sta.uwi.edu/subjects/165}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The only resource found covers Trinidad and Tobago together, so any curriculum would need Tobago-specific data collected from scratch. Further searching should target Tobago House of Assembly education bodies and University of the West Indies linguistics work. I did not find those in this search.
