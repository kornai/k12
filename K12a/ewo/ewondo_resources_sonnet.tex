\section{Introduction}
Ewondo (ISO 639-3 \texttt{ewo}) is a Bantu language of the Centre Region of Cameroon. Searches found a small number of descriptive grammars and bibliographic records but no modern dictionary, no school textbooks and no native-language primers. This report is therefore short; the entries below are bibliographic records located in search results, not verified holdings.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No school primers or textbooks for Ewondo were found in search results. A catalogue-derived list of Cameroonian subject headings mentions a lesson-style course by Abega (\emph{Petit assimil ewondo en 21 leçons}), but it was seen only in one list and should be verified in a library catalogue before use.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Prosper Abega, \emph{La grammaire de l'ewondo} (1969), a grammar in French: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/473084}
\item James E. Redden, \emph{A Descriptive Grammar of Ewondo} (1979), in English: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/146626}
\item Bibliographic records for Bantu grammars in the Reflex/Webball database: \url{https://reflex.cnrs.fr/Lexiques/webball/biblio_bas.php?id=74}
\item Language-archive entry for Ewondo (Cocoon/Huma-Num): \url{https://cocoon.huma-num.fr/exist/crdo/lexvo.xql?code=ewo}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
No audio, video or software resources for Ewondo were found in the search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The available sources are grammars for linguists, not classroom materials. A curriculum would need new primers, a standardised teaching orthography and a dictionary, built on Redden and Abega as reference grammars. A mirror of the Wikipedia article reports concern about displacement of Ewondo by French.
