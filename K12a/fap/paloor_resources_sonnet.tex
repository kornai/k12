\section{Introduction}
Paloor (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fap}; also Palor, Falor, Palar) is a Cangin language of the Atlantic-Congo family spoken in the Thi\`es region of Senegal. The Palor are ethnically Serer but do not speak Serer. Sources give speaker numbers between 5,000 and about 14,500. It is closest to Ndut. No school use was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Paloor is a small, apparently stable community language with only scholarly descriptions available. A curriculum would require new materials, an agreed orthography, and coordination with the Senegalese national language framework, which this search did not examine.
