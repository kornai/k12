\section{Introduction}
Toto (ISO 639-3 txo, Glottocode toto1302) is a Sino-Tibetan language spoken mainly in Totopara, West Bengal, India, near the Bhutan border. It was traditionally oral and has been written in Bengali script. A Toto alphabet was devised by Dhaniram Toto, who received the Padma Shri for this work.
