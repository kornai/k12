\section{Introduction}
Bimin (ISO 639-3 bhl; Glottocode bimi1240) is an Ok language of Papua New Guinea, spoken in Sandaun and Western Provinces between the Murray and Strickland Rivers, with a location noted in the Oksapmin area. It is related to Faiwol, with much intermarriage and cultural exchange with Oksapmin. It is distinct from Mian (Mianmin). Speaker figures in the sources found are about 2,000 (1993) to 2,300 (2003), lower than the roughly 10,000 given in the task; this discrepancy should be checked. Little educational material was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
No evidence of vernacular schooling was found. Development would need orthography and materials work with the community; Oksapmin-area schools and the related Faiwol language may be useful contacts, though no specific programme was found.
