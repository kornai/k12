\section{Introduction}
Atayal (ISO 639-3 tay; Glottocode atay1247) is an endangered Austronesian language of Taiwan. Search results mention school teaching, including extracurricular lessons in Wulai from 1990 and teaching materials developed for specific dialects (S'uli, Matu'uwal). This differs from the reference note that it is not known to be taught in schools.
