\section{Introduction}
Filipino is a national language of the Philippines and a language of instruction. This dossier lists only items seen in search results; it is not exhaustive.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item DepEd Filipino Gabay Pangkurikulum (K to 12 curriculum guide, Grades 1--10, 2016): \url{https://www.depedbohol.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/Filipino-CG.pdf}
\item K to 12 Curriculum Guides listing (Filipino Baitang 1--10): \url{https://tangub.deped.gov.ph/k-to-12-curriculum-guides/}
\item DepEd Learning Resource Management and Development System: \url{https://lrmds.deped.gov.ph/list/latest/kto10?page=521}
\end{itemize}
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Article on the KWF Ortograpiyang Filipino (2014) and the KWF Diksi app: \url{https://depedbataan.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/MANIPESTASYON-AT-PRAKTIKA-NG-ORTOGRAPIYANG-FILIPINO-SA-APLIKASYONG-KWF-DIKSI.pdf}
\item UP Diliman study on loanword spelling rules: \url{https://journals.upd.edu.ph/index.php/socialsciencediliman/article/download/6201/5494}
\end{itemize}
\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None were found in the searches performed.
\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The KWF Diksi app (free dictionary and orthography) is described in the article cited above; no direct app URL was seen.

Official guides date from 2016 and have since been supplemented by MELCs; check DepEd for current versions.
