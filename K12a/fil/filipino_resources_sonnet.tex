\section{Introduction}
Filipino (fil; Austronesian) is an official national language of the Philippines and is taught as a subject in the K to 12 basic education curriculum. This report rests on a single round of web searches and lists only what appeared in the results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item DepEd K to 12 Filipino Curriculum Guide (Grades 1--10), hosted by DepEd Bohol: \url{https://www.depedbohol.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/Filipino-CG.pdf}
\item A news report on the Commission on the Filipino Language (KWF) position on English as a medium of instruction and Filipino in the curriculum (GMA Network): \url{https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/balitambayan/balita/839828/ingles-bilang-midyum-ng-pagtuturo-di-hadlang-sa-pagpapalawig-ng-wikang-filipino-kwf/story/}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Filipino has an established national curriculum guide, so the main gap is not existence of materials but a full inventory of DepEd learning materials, which this short search did not cover.
