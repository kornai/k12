\section{Introduction}
Dhuwaya (ISO 639-3 dwy) is a Yolngu Matha variety of north-east Arnhem Land. Search results describe it as the first language of children at Yirrkala School, where it is used in a long-running bilingual programme.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Yirrkala School runs a staircase-model bilingual programme with Dhuwaya as the main Yolngu language. Search results gave no individual textbook titles or downloadable materials, so those should be requested from the school's Literature Production Centre.
