\section{Introduction}
Bar\'e (ISO 639-3 \texttt{bae}) is an Arawakan language of the upper Rio Negro region of Venezuela and Brazil. Sources disagree on its status: Wikipedia reports the last fluent speaker in Brazil died in 1993, while other figures list a few hundred speakers. Aikhenvald (1999) reports only a few old speakers remaining.
