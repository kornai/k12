\section{Introduction}
Auslan (ISO 639-3 asf) is the Deaf community sign language of Australia. It is an official language option in the Australian Curriculum. The search found no Deaf Australia school materials.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Australian Curriculum: Languages, Auslan (Foundation to Year 10 and Year 7 entry sequences, with support documents; includes a first-language pathway for deaf learners): \url{https://australiancurriculum.edu.au/f-10-curriculum/languages/auslan}
\item Victorian Curriculum Auslan planning, teaching and assessment resources: \url{https://vcaa.vic.edu.au/curriculum/foundation-10/resources/languages/auslan}
\item Twinkl Auslan printable resources for Foundation to Year 2 (quality not verified): \url{https://www.twinkl.com.sa/resources/auslan-languages-f-2-australia}
\end{itemize}
