\section{Introduction}
Chung (ISO 639-3 cnq; also Chungmboko, Cung) is an Eastern Beboid language of Cameroon. Sources give differing speaker counts (about 1,400 in 2001 per Wikipedia; 2,620 per Global Recordings Network; 2,700 per Joshua Project). Very little material exists in the search results, and none suitable for schools was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found. The language is not known to be taught in schools.
