\section{Introduction}
Adangbe (ISO 639-3 adq) is spoken in Ghana and Togo. Sources found in search call it Agotime or Dangbe and place it among the Ghana--Togo Mountain languages. It must not be confused with Adangme (Dangme), a separate Ga-Dangme language. Speaker estimates in the sources vary widely (roughly 1,500 to 14,000). Very little material exists, and no school curriculum material was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is reported as endangered and is not known to be taught in schools. The search found only scripture portions, gospel audio and encyclopedic descriptions. A K-12 curriculum would need new primary documentation, orthography work and community partnership. Local organisations were not identified in the search, so none are named here.
