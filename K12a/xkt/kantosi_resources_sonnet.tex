\section{Introduction}
Kantosi (ISO 639-3 xkt; Glottocode kant1249) is spoken in northern Ghana. Wikipedia classifies it as a Gur language and gives the alternate name Yare (Yarsi). Other names include Kantonsi and Dagaare-Dioula. Speaker estimates vary: 6,300 (Wikipedia, 2020), about 4,200 (Joshua Project) and 3,500 (Global Recordings). Search found very little educational material.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Kantosi were found in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
No evidence of schooling in Kantosi was found. Community-led orthography and materials development would be needed. Relatedness to neighbouring Gur languages of northern Ghana may help, but this was not examined in the search.
