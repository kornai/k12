\section{Introduction}
Kombio (also Endangen; ISO 639-3 xbi) is a Torricelli language of East Sepik Province, Papua New Guinea. Wikipedia gives about 3,000 speakers (2003). Joshua Project places the Kombio people in the Dreikikir district across 31 villages, citing Ethnologue 2016. Speakers are shifting to Tok Pisin. The listed dialects are Mwi, Wampukuamp, Yanimoi and Wampurun, and Mwi is the most divergent. Its position within Torricelli is disputed.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The documentation found is thin: scripture portions in two dialects and encyclopedic descriptions. Dialect differences mean a curriculum would have to choose a standard or develop dialect-specific materials. Fieldwork and community partnership would be needed to supply a dictionary, a grammar and teaching materials.
