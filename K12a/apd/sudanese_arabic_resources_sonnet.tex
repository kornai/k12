\section{Introduction}
Sudanese Arabic (ISO 639-3 apd) is the colloquial variety of Arabic spoken across Sudan. It is largely a spoken language; written education in Sudan uses Modern Standard Arabic. The search returned only a small number of learner-oriented resources.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No school textbooks or primers in Sudanese Arabic were found. \emph{Sudanese Colloquial Arabic for Beginners} was mentioned in a search listing, but I did not verify a usable source for it.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Sudanese Arabic--English / English--Sudanese Arabic: A Concise Dictionary} (Rianne Tamis and co-authors), a dictionary written for foreign learners: \url{https://mabb.kenyaclc.org/product/sudanese-arabic-english-english-sudanese-arabic-a-concise-dictionary/}
\item A resource page listing Bergman's \emph{Spoken Sudanese Arabic: Grammar, Dialogues, and Glossary}, articles, online dictionaries and newspapers: \url{https://universeofmemory.com/sudanese-arabic-language-resources/}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found in the search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The resource page above mentions online dictionaries (Glosbe, Mo3jam). I did not open them.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Sudanese Arabic has few written resources. Any curriculum would need to deal with the relation between the spoken variety and Modern Standard Arabic and would rely largely on new materials.
