\section{Introduction}
This report covers Classical Syriac (ISO 639-3 syc, Glottocode clas1252). The reference description says it is used only as a second language and has no ethnic community. The web search found only English-language materials for adult and university learners. It found no school curriculum and no native-language K-12 materials. Everything below was seen in search results, and no content beyond the search snippets was checked.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primary or secondary school materials turned up. The closest are adult and university course books:
\begin{itemize}
\item Hallam, \emph{Basics of Classical Syriac: Complete Grammar, Workbook, and Lexicon} (2016), a beginner's grammar and workbook. A pack adds a video lecture DVD for each of its 23 chapters. \url{https://zondervanacademic.com/products/basics-of-classical-syriac-pack} and \url{https://logos.com/product/190239/basics-of-classical-syriac-complete-grammar-workbook-and-lexicon}
\item University of Edinburgh course for absolute beginners. It covers the alphabet, grammar and vocabulary. \url{https://www.drps.ed.ac.uk/25-26/dpt/cxdivi11053.htm}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Gorgias Concise Syriac-English, English-Syriac Dictionary, with about 13,000 Syriac-English entries. One Gorgias page marks it as no longer available. \url{https://gorgiaspress.com/gorgias-concise-syriac-english-english-syriac-dictionary}
\item Gorgias Illustrated Learner's Syriac-English, English-Syriac Dictionary (catalogue record). \url{https://catalogo.abie.es/Record/rbagr.507165}
\item A 2019 Hugoye review discusses several Classical Syriac course books (Thackston, Healey, Kiraz, Hallam, Younansardaroud, Robinson). \url{https://hugoye.bethmardutho.org/pdf/vol25/HV25N2PRAim.pdf}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
The search results did not surface scripture editions or community organisations. This section is left empty rather than padded.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
No free online or audio resources appeared in the search. The Gorgias primer is described as coming with downloadable audio, but no direct URL was seen.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Classical Syriac has no community of speakers, and no school programme was found. Any K-12 heritage curriculum would need to be built from the university and adult materials above, so teachers would need to adapt them. Claims about audio, scripture and community resources should be verified separately.
