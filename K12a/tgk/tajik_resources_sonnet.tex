\section{Introduction}
Tajik (ISO 639-3 tgk) is the official national language of Tajikistan and a language of instruction. The search was done in English, so it mainly surfaced learner-oriented and library resources. Native-language school materials are only briefly mentioned, and none were verified directly.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item The learner.net page ``Tajikistan Grade 1 Curriculum - Tajik Language'' states that the Ministry of Education and Science publishes electronic textbooks for grades 1--11 in Tajik-language schools. The page is thin, so the claim should be checked against the Ministry's own site: \url{https://learner.net/u/tajikistan-grade-1-tajik-language}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Hippocrene Tajik-English/English-Tajik Practical Dictionary (about 15,000 entries): \url{https://www.hippocrenebooks.com/store/p318/TajikPracticalDictionary.html}
\item Harvard Caspiana language-resources page, which lists the online dictionaries Durud.tj, Lugat.tj and Sahifa.tj: \url{https://caspiana.omeka.fas.harvard.edu/exhibits/show/academic-resources/language-resources}
\item ACL Anthology paper, EURALI 2022: \url{https://aclanthology.org/2022.eurali-1.9.pdf}
\item \emph{Tajik Persian: Readings in History, Culture and Society} (University of Kansas, 2016): \url{https://catalogue.glasgowkelvin.ac.uk/Record/25550}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Tajik has an established school system, so a curriculum can build on the existing Ministry textbooks. Locating the primary-source Tajik-language materials needs searches in Tajik and Russian, which this search did not cover.
