\section{Introduction}
Kei (Evav; ISO 639-3 kei) is an Austronesian language of the Kei Islands, Maluku, Indonesia, with about 85,000 speakers. There is no official orthography. The search found only general reference pages, not a dictionary or school materials.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The lack of a standard orthography and of published teaching materials means curriculum work would begin with orthography standardization and a lexicon, with Indonesian as the likely bridge language. Kei Kecil and Kei Besar dialects differ.
