\section{Introduction}
Casuarina Coast Asmat (ISO 639-3 asc) is spoken along the Casuarina Coast of South Papua, Indonesia, and is the most divergent of the Asmat languages. Web searches found no school materials, no modern dictionary and no post-1980 grammar. Only a few resources are listed below.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in search results.
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Voorhoeve (1980), \emph{The Asmat Languages of Irian Jaya}: a comparative overview with wordlists, not a learner's grammar. Catalogue record: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/85998}
\item TransNewGuinea.org database, with Casuarina Coast dialect wordlist entries drawn from Voorhoeve: \url{https://transnewguinea.org/word/crab}
\item Overview article: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casuarina_Coast_Asmat_language}
\end{itemize}
\section{Religious and Community Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Scripture Earth listing for asc, pointing to a Bible text on YouVersion and Global Recordings Network audio: \url{https://scriptureearth.org/00eng.php?iso=asc}
\end{itemize}
\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Audio recordings are reached through the Scripture Earth page above. No other digital resources were found.
\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools. A curriculum would have to start from the Voorhoeve wordlists and the scripture text, and would need new documentation work with the community.
