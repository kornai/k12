\section{Introduction}
North Tanna (ISO 639-3 tnn; Glottocode nort2847) is an Austronesian language spoken on the northern coast of Tanna Island, Vanuatu, with about 5,000 speakers. Its relation to neighbouring Tanna languages such as Whitesands and Lenakel is not settled. The search found very little specific to North Tanna.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found. The reference description says the language is taught as a subject in some schools, but I found no source for this.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Documentation is thin. The Sverredal grammar sketch is the only North Tanna description reported. Related-language materials for Lenakel and Whitesands could inform an orthography, but they are not North Tanna and should be treated with care.
