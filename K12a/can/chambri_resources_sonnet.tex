\section{Introduction}
Chambri (ISO 639-3 \texttt{can}, Glottocode cham1313) is a Lower Sepik language spoken in the Chambri Lakes region of East Sepik Province, Papua New Guinea. Wikipedia lists about 800 native speakers (2006), lower than the figure in the prompt. No Chambri dictionary or school materials turned up in the searches.
