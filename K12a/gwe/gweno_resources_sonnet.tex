\section{Introduction}
Gweno (Kigweno, ISO 639-3 gwe) is a Bantu language of the North Pare Mountains, Kilimanjaro Region, Tanzania. Search results describe it as endangered and mostly spoken by older adults, with younger people shifting to Asu and Swahili; it is described as unwritten. Very little teaching material exists online.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
With the language held mainly by older speakers, a curriculum would depend on new documentation work. The Sewangi lexicon and the Philippson and Nurse study are the main references identified; Swahili and English materials would carry most instruction.
