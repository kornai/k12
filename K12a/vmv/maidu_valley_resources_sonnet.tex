\section{Introduction}
Valley Maidu (ISO 639-3 vmv; Glottolog vall1252) is an extinct Maiduan language of California. Searches found no Valley Maidu dictionary or revitalization programme. The nearest well-documented relative found is Konkow Maidu.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None were found specifically for Valley Maidu. The Konkow Maidu site below includes lessons and stories for a related variety. Whether they are usable for Valley Maidu was not verified.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little Valley Maidu material was found. A curriculum would probably rest on archival sources, such as the early recordings and notes held in archives, together with Maiduan community partners. Konkow Maidu materials could serve as a model. This is a limited basis.
