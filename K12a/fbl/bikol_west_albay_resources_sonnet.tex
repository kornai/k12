\section{Introduction}
West Albay Bikol (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fbl}) is a Bikol variety of Albay province, Philippines, spoken in a group of towns (for example Oas, Libon, Polangui) between the Rinconada area and Legazpi. Each town's speech differs noticeably. The searches found no dedicated West Albay dictionary, primer or full grammar.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is described as vigorous, but no school programme in it was found. Materials for standard (Naga-based) Bikol, which is outside this survey entry, may serve as a model only. New materials would have to be written, and a town variety chosen.
