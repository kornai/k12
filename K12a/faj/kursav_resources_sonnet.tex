\section{Introduction}
Kursav (ISO 639-3: faj; also called Faita or Kulsab) is a nearly extinct language of the Madang family, spoken on the south bank of the upper Ramu River in Madang Province, Papua New Guinea. Wikipedia reports about 10 speakers as of 2015 and UNESCO classes it as severely endangered; older speakers use Gende and younger people speak only Tok Pisin. No school materials were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Kursav were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
With only a handful of elderly speakers, any work is language documentation and revitalisation rather than a full K-12 curriculum. Tok Pisin and Gende are the languages of daily use. The archive records above are the likeliest source of recordings.
