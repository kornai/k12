\section{Introduction}
Tarok is a Tarokoid (Benue-Congo) language of southeastern Plateau State, Nigeria. It is a lingua franca of the southern Plateau region. Wikipedia estimates about 520,000 people. The reference description gives about 300,000 speakers.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
The searches found no primers or school textbooks for Tarok.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Selbut Longtau, \textit{The Tarok Language: Its Basic Principles and Grammar} (KWEF, 2009), a 241-page grammar in English: \url{https://www.koeppe.de/titel_the-tarok-language}
\item Wikipedia, Tarok language, with links to Longtau's spelling and phonology documents: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarok_language}
\item Blench, 2000, listed in Glottolog: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/134950}
\item Study of Tarok young people's speech: \url{https://api.equinoxpub.com/articles/28316}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Tarok Bible on eBible.org (CC BY-SA 4.0, contributed by The Seed Company, 2024 copyright held by the Luke Initiative for Scripture Translation): \url{https://ebible.org/details.php?id=yer}
\item About page for the Tarok Bible: \url{https://ebible.org/study/content/texts/yer/about.html}
\item Joshua Project language record, which lists a New Testament from 1988--2007 and reports no complete Bible. It may predate the eBible text: \url{https://www.joshuaproject.net/index.php/languages/yer}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The Bible text and Longtau's grammar and orthography notes are the main materials found. No dictionary or primer turned up in these searches. Further searching is needed in SIL and Nigerian Bible translation publications.
