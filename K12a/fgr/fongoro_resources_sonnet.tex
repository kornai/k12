\section{Introduction}
Fongoro (ISO 639-3: fgr; also Gele, Kole) is a severely endangered language of eastern Chad (Ouadda\"i region, Sila department, Fongoro canton, along the Sudan border), described as Central Sudanic of uncertain classification. Sources report only a few elderly speakers. Glottolog argues that the available lexical data show it to be a dialectal variant of Gula (kcm). Very little exists beyond brief reference entries, and nothing suitable for schools was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
With only a few elderly speakers and an unsettled classification, a school curriculum in Fongoro is not currently feasible from published sources. Any effort would begin with documentation work with remaining speakers and with the Gula question. Regional languages (Arabic, Fur) are the practical media of instruction.
