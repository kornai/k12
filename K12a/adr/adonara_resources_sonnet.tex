\section{Introduction}
Adonara (ISO 639-3 adr) is spoken on Adonara Island, East Flores Regency, Indonesia. Linguists treat it as part of the Lamaholot dialect cluster (Central Malayo-Polynesian), and its classification is debated. Most scholarship covers Lamaholot varieties generally. Little is specific to Adonara.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is stable with many speakers but is not known to be taught in schools. Existing documentation is a grammar sketch of western Lamaholot dialects and a dialect-variation study. A curriculum would first need a lexicon, an agreed orthography and local teacher input. No local organisations were identified in the search.
