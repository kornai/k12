\section{Introduction}
Pahanan Agta (ISO 639-3: apf; also called Paranan Agta or Palanan Agta) is spoken by a Negrito community in Palanan, Isabela, in the northern Philippines. Wikipedia gives about 1,700 speakers as of 2009. It is lexically very close to the unrelated-in-structure Paranan language. Few resources were found, and none designed for schools.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Material in the language appears limited to scripture portions. No dictionary, grammar or school material was found, so a curriculum would require new documentation work with the community.
