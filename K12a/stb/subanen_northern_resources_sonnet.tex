\section{Introduction}
Northern Subanen (ISO 639-3: stb) is an Austronesian language of the Philippines, part of the Subanen dialect cluster in Zamboanga del Norte and neighbouring provinces. The search found nothing specific to Northern Subanen. The results covered the Subanen group in general and the related Western Subanon variety. Nothing below should be taken as a Northern Subanen resource unless it says so.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No Northern Subanen primers, textbooks or school materials were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Northern Subanen is stable and is the first language of the whole ethnic community, but it is not known to be taught in schools. Documentation specific to this variety was not found. A curriculum project would need to locate the dissertations and mission or government materials on the Subanen varieties through library searches, and work with Subanen educators. Materials for Western Subanon might help, but they are a different variety.
