\section{Introduction}
Europanto (ISO 639-3 eur, deprecated) is a humorous macaronic ``language'' created in 1996 by Diego Marani, a translator for the Council of the European Union. It mixes European vocabulary with loose, speaker-chosen grammar. According to Wikipedia, Marani was reportedly no longer actively promoting it as of 2005. It has no native speakers, no fixed grammar and no standard orthography, so a comprehensive K-12 curriculum conducted in Europanto is not a meaningful goal. This dossier records only what was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Europanto could at most serve as a classroom example of language play, code-mixing or European multilingualism, within a language-awareness unit. It is not suited to being a language of instruction.
