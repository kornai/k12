\section{Introduction}
Portuguese Sign Language (L\'ingua Gestual Portuguesa, LGP; ISO 639-3 psr) is the language of the Portuguese Deaf community. This dossier is based on one brief search; items were seen as search-result titles and summaries and should be checked.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Programa curricular de L\'ingua Gestual Portuguesa (pre-school and basic education), DGE: \url{https://www.dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/ficheiros/programacurricularlgportuguesa.pdf}
\item Programa curricular de LGP, secondary education, DGE: \url{https://dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/EEspecial/prog_curric_lgp_sec.pdf}
\item Programa de Portugu\^es L\'ingua Segunda for Deaf students (two versions): \url{https://www.dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/ficheiros/programa_portugueslingua2_1_.pdf} and \url{https://dge.mec.pt/sites/default/files/EEspecial/programa_pl2_surdos.pdf}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Portuguese bilingual education for Deaf students began in schools from 1997 and was regulated by Decree-Law 3/2008, according to the articles found. The model treats LGP as the first language and written Portuguese as the second. Articles: \url{https://seer.ines.gov.br/index.php/revista-espaco/article/download/1266/1282/4041}, \url{https://periodicos.furg.br/momento/article/download/14189/9687/49638}, \url{https://e-revista.unioeste.br/index.php/ideacao/article/download/17226/11544/63299}
