\section{Introduction}
Zimbabwe Sign Language (ISO 639-3 zib, Glottocode zimb1247) is the language of the Deaf community in Zimbabwe. It was recognised officially in the 2013 Constitution. Search results show policy and research literature but no dedicated dictionary or curriculum materials.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Policy support exists (constitutional recognition, and a stated intention to teach sign language as a subject). Studies report that teachers lack training and teaching resources. A curriculum would need a signed dictionary and signed video materials, produced with Deaf community leadership.
