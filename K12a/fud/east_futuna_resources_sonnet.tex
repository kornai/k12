\section{Introduction}
East Futuna (ISO 639-3: fud; Glottocode: east2447) is an Austronesian (Polynesian) language of Wallis and Futuna, with about 3,600 speakers. It is also called Futunan or East Futunan, to distinguish it from West Futuna (Futuna-Aniwa) of Vanuatu, a different language. This search found reference and linguistic materials only; no school materials were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school curricula in East Futuna were found in the search results. Teaching materials would have to be sought through the education authorities of Wallis and Futuna, which this search did not reach.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Materials found are scholarly and French-oriented. A native-language K-12 curriculum would need new primers and readers, built from the linguistic descriptions above and in cooperation with local speakers. The extent of existing school provision remains unverified.
