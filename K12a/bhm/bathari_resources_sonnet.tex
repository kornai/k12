\section{Introduction}
Bathari (Ba\d{t}\d{h}ari, ISO 639-3 bhm) is a Modern South Arabian language of the Afro-Asiatic family, spoken on the south-eastern coast of Oman. Sources in the search results describe it as nearly extinct, with very few speakers (Wikipedia gives 16 native speakers for 2016), and as closely related to Mehri; some members of the Bathari tribe speak only Mehri. No school materials in or for Bathari were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
