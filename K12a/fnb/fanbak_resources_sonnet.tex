\section{Introduction}
Fanbak (Fanbyak, Orkon; ISO 639-3 \texttt{fnb}) is an endangered Austronesian language of northern Ambrym, Vanuatu. Speaker estimates range from about 95 to about 130. The village of Fanbyak is abandoned and speakers live mainly among North Ambrym speakers. Whether the Orkon variety was a separate lect is unsettled.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is documented by researchers but not taught. A curriculum would depend on community consultation and on the archived documentation, whose access conditions were not checked.
