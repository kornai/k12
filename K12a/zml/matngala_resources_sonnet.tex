\section{Introduction}
Matngala (also Matngele, Madngele, Warat; ISO 639-3 zml, Glottocode madn1237) is an Eastern Daly language formerly spoken on the middle and lower Daly River, Northern Territory. It is extinct or nearly so. A report from 1983 gave 15 to 20 speakers. Its place within Eastern Daly is disputed.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The documentation is thin: one grammar sub-thesis, a few recordings and some wordlists. A curriculum would be mainly cultural and heritage content, developed with Daly River community members. Any archival audio would need access permission.
