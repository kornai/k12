\section{Introduction}
Huizhou Chinese (ISO 639-3 \texttt{czh}) is a group of closely related Sinitic varieties spoken in and around the historical Huizhou region, mostly in southern Anhui, with roughly 4.6 million speakers in the figures supplied. It should not be confused with the Guangdong Huizhou dialect (Bendihua), a different variety.
