\section{Introduction}
Fe'fe' (also Nufi, Bafang; ISO 639-3 \texttt{fmp}) is a Bamileke language of the West Region of Cameroon, with about 140,000 speakers around Bafang. Searches found a Latin-script orthography and some reference works, but no modern comprehensive school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No current primers or textbooks were found in search results. Omniglot reports that French missionaries began writing the language in the 1920s and that it was later taught in schools and used in a newspaper; that history is a lead for finding older materials.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A curriculum could build on the AGLC-based orthography and the older missionary-era literacy tradition. Tone is highly contrastive and must be handled in any literacy materials. Contacting Cameroonian language committees would be needed; none were identified in these searches.
