\section{Introduction}
Baduy (ISO 639-3 \texttt{bac}) is spoken by the Kanekes community in the mountains of western Java, Indonesia. It is conventionally treated as a Sundanese dialect but often considered a separate language. It has no official writing system. The 2015 census recorded 11,620 speakers according to Wikipedia.
