\section{Introduction}
Borgu Fulfulde (ISO 639-3: fue; Glottocode: borg1235) is a Fula variety spoken mainly in the Borgou region of Benin. It uses a Latin script and has dialects including Bakuure, Korakuure, Juguure and Caabankeere. It is not known to be taught in schools. The search results show mostly religious materials, and no dictionary, grammar or educational material was found.
