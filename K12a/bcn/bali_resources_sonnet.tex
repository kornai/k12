\section{Introduction}
Bali (ISO 639-3 bcn; Glottocode bali1245), also called Bali (Adamawa), Kibala or Kibali, is a Niger-Congo language of Nigeria spoken in Demsa, Numan and Mayo Belwa areas of Adamawa State and Lau area of Taraba State. Wikipedia gives about 100,000 speakers (2006). It must not be confused with Nga'ka (mhk), the language of Bali Nyonga in Cameroon, which is also called Bali, nor with Nyong (Bali-Kumbat).
