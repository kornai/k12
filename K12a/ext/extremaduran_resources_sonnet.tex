\section{Introduction}
Extremaduran (\emph{estreme\~nu}, ISO 639-3 \texttt{ext}) is an endangered Romance variety of western Spain. Search results showed some orthographic, grammatical and lexicographic work but nothing on classroom teaching or textbooks. Its status as a language rather than a dialect, and its lack of settled spelling rules, are disputed.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No school textbooks or teaching programmes were found in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The Mercator research page on Extremaduran in Spain gives the legal and educational context: \url{https://wiki.mercator-research.eu/languages:extremaduran_in_spain}. Speaker estimates vary widely and use is concentrated among older adults, so any curriculum would start from a small base of standardised materials.
