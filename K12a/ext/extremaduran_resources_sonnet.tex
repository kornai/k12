\section{Introduction}
Extremaduran (\emph{estreme\~nu}, ISO 639-3 \texttt{ext}) is an endangered Western Romance language of Extremadura and adjacent areas of Spain. This report lists only what a web search turned up.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No school textbooks or primers were found in the search results, and no school programme was identified.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Wikipedia article on the language: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremaduran_language}
\item Omniglot page (alphabet and orthography overview, mentions the 2017 \emph{Ortograf\'ia del Extreme\~no} by Ismael Carmona Garc\'ia): \url{https://omniglot.com/writing/extremaduran.htm}
\item Mercator research wiki, Extremaduran in Spain: \url{https://wiki.mercator-research.eu/languages:extremaduran_in_spain}
\item University of Extremadura repository item found in search (content not verified): \url{https://dehesa.unex.es/handle/10662/14942?locale=en}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None identified. The search mentioned the OSCEC, a body founded in 2011 for standardisation, but no URL for it was seen.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Extremaduran Wikipedia (about 4,000 articles): \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremaduran_Wikipedia}
\end{itemize}
A community word list (\emph{Izionariu}) and short grammar were mentioned in a summary, but no verified URL was seen; no audio was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools, and standardisation is recent and contested among varieties. The 2017 orthography is the likeliest base for materials; the primary publication should be located and checked.
