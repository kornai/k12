\section{Introduction}
Guibei Zhuang (zgb) is a Zhuang variety of China, within the Kra-Dai family. The search was narrow, and it found mainly audio gospel material. It found no school resources, and the codes zgb, zgn and zyb are easy to confuse. Check the code before relying on any Bible or school resource for ``Zhuang''.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes specific to Guibei Zhuang were found in search.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
No grammar or dictionary specific to Guibei Zhuang was found in search.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Guibei Zhuang has many speakers, but search found no materials specific to it. Standard Zhuang is the written form in China. A curriculum would have to decide how to relate local Guibei speech to that standard, and would need local expertise.
