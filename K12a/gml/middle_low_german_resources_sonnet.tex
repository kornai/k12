\section{Introduction}
Middle Low German (ISO 639-3 gml) was the written language of the Hanseatic region, roughly 1200--1650. It has no native speakers today. Searches found scholarly dictionaries and a reference corpus but no school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in search results.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item August L\"ubben, \emph{Mittelniederdeutsches Handw\"orterbuch} (1888), free edition: \url{https://gutenberg.org/ebooks/61948}; PDF scan: \url{https://www.koeblergerhard.de/Fontes/Luebben_MittelniederdeutschesHandwoerterbuch1888.pdf}.
\item Sch\"iller--L\"ubben, \emph{Mittelniederdeutsches W\"orterbuch} (1875--1881), the larger parent dictionary; scan of the supplement volume: \url{https://www.archive.org/download/bub_gb_ZokNAAAAQAAJ/bub_gb_ZokNAAAAQAAJ.pdf}.
\item Agathe Lasch's 1914 grammar remains the standard first reference, according to a Peter Lang source found in search: \url{https://www.peterlang.com/document/1302276}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Materials found are for researchers. Any school curriculum would need adapted readers built from the corpus and dictionaries. No audio was found.
