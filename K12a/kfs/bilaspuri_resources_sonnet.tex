\section{Introduction}
Bilaspuri (Kahluri; ISO 639-3 kfs; Glottocode bila1253) is a Western Pahari language of Bilaspur district, Himachal Pradesh, with about 300,000 speakers (2011), counted in the census under Punjabi. Very little dedicated material was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Bilaspuri were found in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is written today mainly in Devanagari, historically in a Takri variant. Without published teaching materials, a curriculum would depend on new fieldwork and community input.
