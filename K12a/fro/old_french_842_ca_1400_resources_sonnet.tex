\section{Introduction}
Old French (842--ca.\ 1400) has no native speakers. Available materials are university-level; nothing school-level was found in search results.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Lexicity wiki guide to Old French, listing online lessons (10 lessons), verb charts and text databases (community wiki, informal): \url{https://oderalon.net/wiki/index.php/Lexicity/Franceis_Old_French}
\item Kibler, \emph{An Introduction to Old French} (reading selections, e.g.\ Marie de France, with morphology and syntax): \url{https://backstory.london/products/9780873522922}
\end{itemize}
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Einhorn, \emph{Old French: A Concise Handbook} (phonology, morphology, syntax): \url{https://www.langcen.cam.ac.uk/resources/langf/data/fr_101.html}
\item Einhorn, \emph{Old French} (e-book listing): \url{https://www.redshelf.com/book/191915/old-french-191915-9780511864643-e-einhorn}
\item Luquiens, \emph{An Introduction to Old French Phonology and Morphology}: \url{https://www.thriftbooks.com/w/an-introduction-to-old-french-phonology-and-morphology_frederick-bliss-luquiens/50285928/}
\item Dictionnaire du moyen fran\c{c}ais (1330--1500; covers only the late period): \url{https://mdr-maa.org/resource/dictionnaire-du-moyen-francais-1330-1500/}
\end{itemize}
\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found.
\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The Lexicity page points to the Anglo-Norman Texts database; see the wiki guide above. No audio was found.
\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A K-12 heritage programme would need to adapt university materials, for example short readings from the texts above. No existing school curriculum was found.
