\section{Introduction}
Carolina Algonquian (ISO 639-3 crr) is extinct. Our knowledge rests on word lists made around Roanoke in 1585--86, chiefly by Thomas Harriot, and on later notes by John Lawson. No curriculum materials were found.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found in search results. A heritage curriculum would have to be reconstructed from the historical record.
