\section{Introduction}
Western Neo-Aramaic (ISO 639-3 amw) is spoken in Ma'lula and Jubb'adin in Syria (Bakh'a is reported depopulated). Sources disagree on speaker numbers. This report lists what appeared in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
Search results mention Anas Abou-Ismail, \emph{Western Neo-Aramaic: The Dialect of Jubaadin} (Cambridge Scholars Publishing, 2020), an English textbook; no URL for it was seen. No school programme was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Printed scholarship exists, but community-facing teaching materials are not evident. Conflict has displaced speakers, so any curriculum work needs speaker partners.
