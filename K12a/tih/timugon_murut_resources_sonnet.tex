\section{Introduction}
Timugon Murut (ISO 639-3: tih) is an Austronesian language of Sabah, Malaysia. Sources disagree on speaker numbers (about 7,000 in the survey description; about 15,000 on Webonary; fewer than 10,000 in a news report). Two main dialects, Poros and Kapagalan, are described, with the related Beaufort Murut (Binta'). No school programme in the language was found. The best resource is the online dictionary.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
