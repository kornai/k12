\section{Introduction}
Poqomam (ISO 639-3 poc; Glottocode poqo1253) is a Mayan language of Guatemala with about 9,500 speakers, rated threatened by the Endangered Languages Project. The search found a small number of dictionary resources and little classroom material.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item ALMG Poqomam--Spanish bilingual dictionary (published December 2024), offered for learners and for strengthening the language; news report: \url{https://agn.gt/?p=402251}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Glottolog entry listing over 50 references, including dictionaries and grammar sketches: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/poqo1253}
\item WALS language page (the grammar \emph{Gram\'atica del idioma poqomam}, Santos Nicol\'as et al., 1997, is cited there; no digital copy was found): \url{https://wals.info/languoid/lect/wals_code_pcm}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
No religious or community-organisation resources were identified in the searches.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Poqomam Living Dictionary, with English and Spanish glosses and audio: \url{https://livingdictionaries.app/poqomam}
\item Endangered Languages Project entry (talking dictionary by the Living Tongues Institute is listed there): \url{https://endangeredlanguages.com/node/97286}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools, and no textbooks or primers were found. The ALMG dictionary and the Living Dictionary provide vocabulary and audio; the 1997 grammar would be the main reference for orthography and structure if a copy can be obtained through ALMG.
