\section{Introduction}
Semimi (ISO 639-3 etz; also called Etna Bay) is a Papuan language of the Mairasic family, spoken in Teluk Etna District, Kaimana Regency, West Papua, Indonesia. Published figures are about 1,000 speakers (a 1991 count cited by Wikipedia) and about 1,500 people (Joshua Project). The search found only encyclopedic and wordlist-level material. No primer, textbook, school programme or native-language publication turned up.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No educational materials for Semimi were found in the search results. The language is not known to be taught in schools.
