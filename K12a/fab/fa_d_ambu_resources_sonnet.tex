\section{Introduction}
Fa d’Ambu (Fa d’Amb\^o, Annobonese Creole; ISO 639-3 \texttt{fab}) is a Portuguese-based creole of Annobón island, Equatorial Guinea, with communities on Bioko and the mainland. It is related to the creoles of São Tomé and Príncipe. Speaker estimates range from about 2,500 to 6,600 depending on source. Per a 2021 language snapshot by Agostinho, it is the mother tongue of all Annobónese, with no language planning, education policy or standardization.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is vital in the community but unstandardized and absent from education. Curriculum development would need an agreed orthography; the academic articles above are the best starting point. Related São Tomé creoles (Forro, Angolar) are distinct languages.
