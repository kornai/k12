\section{Introduction}
West Damar (ISO 639-3 \texttt{drn}; Glottocode west2548), also called Damar Batumerah, is an Austronesian language of Damar Island, Maluku, Indonesia, spoken in two villages on the northwest coast (about 800 speakers). Descriptive coverage is thin.
