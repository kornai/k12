\section{Introduction}
Yapese (ISO 639-3 yap; Austronesian) is spoken in Yap State, Federated States of Micronesia. This report lists only resources that appeared in search results. The searches turned up no current Yap State curriculum or official dictionary.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Yaat nu Waqab} (``Tales of Yap''), a 1978 Yapese vernacular reader printed by the Yap District Department of Education. It is catalogued at the University of Hawai\textquotesingle i ScholarSpace. I could not tell which of the two ScholarSpace links below is its record, so check both.
\url{https://hdl.handle.net/10125/39969}
\url{https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/e96eb3a9-b191-432b-9d18-953ee7031b67/full}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Yapese language overview (Wikipedia), including notes on orthography. \url{https://wikipedia.com/wiki/ISO_639:yap}
\end{itemize}
The search summary also mentions a Yapese dictionary and texts collection (John Jensen) on PARADISEC. I did not see its URL, so there is no link.

None found in these searches.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
