\section{Introduction}
Khumi Awa Chin (ISO 639-3 cka) is a Kuki-Chin language of Paletwa Township, Chin State, Myanmar, with some speakers in Bangladesh. Sources found do not separate Awa clearly from the wider Khumi Chin language (cnk), so some items below concern Khumi generally. Little exists specifically for Awa.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found. No school primers, textbooks or curriculum for Awa Khumi appeared in the searches.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Khumi language overview (Wikipedia). \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khumi_language}
\end{itemize}
No dictionary or grammar was found.

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
An Asia Harvest profile lists a Khumi Bible (2014), New Testament (1959) and portions (1935), with about 12 dialects; it concerns Khumi in general and does not necessarily cover Awa. \url{https://www.operation.asiaharvest.org/MYANMAR-Khumi.pdf}

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Global Recordings Network, ``Chin, Khumi: Awa'' audio and audio-visual Bible lessons. \url{https://globalrecordings.net/en/language/1150}
\item Global Recordings Network, Chin, Khumi (general). \url{https://globalrecordings.net/en/language/cnk}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Resources are scarce and mostly religious or audio-only. A curriculum would require new community-led development of orthography, readers and materials.
