\section{Introduction}
Northwestern Fars (ISO 639-3 \texttt{faz}) appears not to be a real language. Ethnologue states that it could not identify any evidence for its existence and is pursuing removal of the code from the standard. Glottolog notes that every candidate language matching the name already has another entry, including Sivandi (\texttt{siy}) and several Kurdish and Gurani varieties. I found no official notice confirming that the removal has been completed.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
There is no language community to serve under this code. Curriculum work for the area should instead consider the languages Glottolog suggests as the real referents, such as Sivandi and the Kurdish and Gurani varieties, each under its own code.
