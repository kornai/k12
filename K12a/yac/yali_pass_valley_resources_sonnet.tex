\section{Introduction}
Pass Valley Yali (ISO 639-3: yac; Glottocode pass1247) is a Trans-New Guinea language of Papua, Indonesia. It is also called Abenaho, North Ngalik or Western Yali. Joshua Project lists about 8,300 speakers. Ethnologue treats it as separate from Ninia Yali (nlk) and Angguruk Yali (yli). Do not confuse it with those varieties.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Audio recordings are reachable through the Scripture Earth page above. No other digital resources were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Very little is documented. The language is endangered and not known to be taught in schools. Scripture and a few reference pages are the only materials found, so a curriculum would depend on new community-led work.
