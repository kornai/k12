\section{Introduction}
Acatepec Me'phaa (ISO 639-3 tpx; Glottocode acat1239) is an Otomanguean language spoken in highland Guerrero, Mexico. It is one of several Me'phaa (Tlapanec) varieties. I found no textbook or dictionary published specifically for the Acatepec variety. Most materials are for Me'phaa or Tlapanec in general, and the catalogues do not say which variety they cover.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item UNICEF Mexico, \emph{Aprendiendo en Me'phaa o Tlapaneco}, bilingual educational materials: \url{https://www.unicef.org/mexico/informes/aprendiendo-en-tlapaneco}
\item \emph{Ajng\'aa me' phaa: lengua tlapaneca, Guerrero, primer ciclo: libro de lecturas} (1995), a catalogue record at UPN (Universidad Pedag\'ogica Nacional). The variety is not stated. \url{https://sibi.upn.mx/bib/99456}
\item \emph{Programa de lengua me'phaa}, a 2015 catalogue record for Chilpancingo, Guerrero, at UPN. The variety is not stated. \url{https://sibi.upn.mx/bib/203465}
\item Two further UPN catalogue records, which I could not identify more precisely: \url{https://sibi.upn.mx/bib/122690} and \url{https://sibi.upn.mx/bib/200810}
\end{itemize}

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The search found no audio resources. The Kansas dictionary project, if completed, would be the main digital resource.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Acatepec Me'phaa has a large speaker base, but I found no materials specific to the Acatepec variety. A curriculum could start from the SEP and UNICEF Me'phaa materials, adapted to local usage. INALI or SEP could be asked about Acatepec-specific materials, and the Kansas dictionary team could be asked about its status.
