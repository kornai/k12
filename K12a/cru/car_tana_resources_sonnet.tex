\section{Introduction}
Carútana (ISO 639-3 \texttt{cru}) is treated in the sources found as a variety closely related to, or a dialect of, Baniwa (Karu group, Arawakan). Its status is contested: Kaufman (1994) treats the Karu varieties as distinct languages, while Aikhenvald (1999) treats them as dialects. No Carútana-specific educational material turned up in the searches; what exists is general reference material on Baniwa.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little is documented for Carútana itself. Any curriculum work would likely need to build on Baniwa/Kuripako materials and on fieldwork with the community, after confirming how speakers themselves define the variety.
