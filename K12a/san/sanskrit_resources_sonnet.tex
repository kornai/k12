\section{Introduction}
Sanskrit (ISO 639-3 \texttt{san}; Glottocode sans1269) is an Indo-European language of India, used mainly as a second and liturgical language. It is taught as a school subject. This search was narrow and covered only the NCERT/CBSE textbook series, so more resources certainly exist.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
NCERT publishes the \emph{Ruchira} Sanskrit textbook series (Bhag 1 to 3) for the upper primary grades, and CBSE schools use it. The pages found are third-party study sites, not NCERT itself, so the details are unverified. The official NCERT site (ncert.nic.in) is the authoritative source, but it did not appear in the results.
\begin{itemize}
\item Vedantu page for the Class 6 Ruchira book (15 chapters): \url{https://vedantu.com/ncert-books/ncert-books-class-6-sanskrit-ruchira}
\item Vedantu page for the Class 8 Ruchira book: \url{https://www.vedantu.com/ncert-books/ncert-books-class-8-sanskrit-ruchira}
\item Arihant workbook based on the NCERT Ruchira Part 3: \url{https://arihantbooks.com/products/ncert-pathyepustak-per-aadharit-sanskrit-workbook-ruchira-trityo-bhag}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Sanskrit has an established school curriculum in India. The NCERT textbooks are the obvious starting point, but their texts mostly use Hindi or English for instruction. A curriculum that uses Sanskrit as the medium would need further searching, for example of Samskrita Bharati and the Sanskrit-medium schools.
