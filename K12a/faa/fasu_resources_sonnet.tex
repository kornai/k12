\section{Introduction}
Fasu (also Namo Me, ``real speech''; ISO 639-3 \texttt{faa}) is a Kutubuan language of Papua New Guinea, spoken southwest of Lake Kutubu in the Southern Highlands and Western provinces, with about 1,200 speakers (Usher, 1981 figure).
