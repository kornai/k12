\section{Introduction}
Harsusi (ISO 639-3 hss) is a Modern South Arabian language of Oman with roughly 500 to 1,500 speakers according to the sources found. Documentation is thin, and no school materials were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found in search results.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Harry Stroomer, \emph{Harsusi Texts from Oman} (Harrassowitz, 2004), the texts underlying T.M. Johnstone's Harsusi Lexicon (listing page). \url{https://reado.app/en/book/harsusi-texts-from-oman-harry-stroomer/9783447050975}
\item Endangered Languages Project entry listing a short Harsusi lexicon. \url{https://endangeredlanguages.com/node/95881}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found in search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The ELDP documentation collection (Eades and Morris, University of Salford) covers Harsusi as spoken in Jiddat al-Harasis, with audio and audio-visual data, transcriptions, translations and a planned comparative cultural glossary; I could not confirm how much is complete or openly accessible. \url{https://eldp.access.preservica.com/dk0314/}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Very little exists. Any curriculum would depend on the archived documentation and on work with the speaker community.
