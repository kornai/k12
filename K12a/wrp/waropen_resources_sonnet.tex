\section{Introduction}
Waropen (ISO 639-3 wrp; Glottocode waro1242) is an endangered Austronesian language of Indonesia. Searches found little material. No school curriculum, primer, textbook or community programme turned up, and no modern dictionary or recent grammar either.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school materials in Waropen were found in search results.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Gerrit Held, \emph{Grammatica van het Waropensch} (Bandung, 1942), written in Dutch. It is cited by the Austronesian Comparative Dictionary, and I did not see a URL for the work itself. It appears to be the most substantial published grammar.
\item Flassy, Animung and Sawaki (1994), \emph{Fonologi bahasa Waropen}, a phonology in Indonesian. I did not see a URL for it.
\item Wikipedia entry for Waropen: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waropen_language}
\item Austronesian Comparative Dictionary, Waropen page (cognate sets): \url{https://acd.clld.org/languages/439}
\item Austronesian Basic Vocabulary Database, Waropen wordlist: \url{https://abvd.eva.mpg.de/austronesian/language.php?id=142}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
No scripture translations or community organisations were found in search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
No audio, video or software resources were found. The online wordlists above are the only digital items seen. Verbix has a short verb list: \url{https://docs.verbix.com/Languages/Waropen}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little exists to build on. A curriculum would need new documentation work with speakers, starting from the Held grammar and the wordlists. Indonesian-language sources may exist, but none were found here.
