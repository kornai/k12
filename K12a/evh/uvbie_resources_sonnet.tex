\section{Introduction}
Uvbie (also Uvwie, Uvwi\d{e}; ISO 639-3 \texttt{evh}, Glottocode uvbi1238) is spoken by the Uvwie people in Delta State, Nigeria, with a reported (dated) figure of about 20,000 speakers. Very little exists; no school materials were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is close to Urhobo, for which more material exists, but that was not verified here and Urhobo resources are not listed as Uvbie resources. A curriculum would start from orthography development.
