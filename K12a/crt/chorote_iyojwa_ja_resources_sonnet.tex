\section{Introduction}
Iyojwa'ja Chorote (ISO 639-3 crt; Glottocode iyoj1235) is a Mataguayan language of Salta province, Argentina. The search results show scholarly documentation but no school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Chorote--Spanish dictionary (Iyojwa'ja variety) in the ELDP archive: \url{https://eldp.access.preservica.com/dk0080/}
\item Ana Gerzenstein, \emph{Lengua chorote} (1978--79), Glottolog record: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/14118}
\item Ana Gerzenstein (1976), descriptive grammar, Glottolog record: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/548985}
\item ``Esbozo fonol\'ogico del chorote (mataguayo)'', phonological sketch: \url{https://sumarios.org/artigo/esbozo-fonol%C3%B3gico-del-chorote-mataguayo}
\item SAPhon phoneme inventory for Chorote (Iyo'wujwa and Iyowa'ja dialects): \url{https://linguistics.berkeley.edu/saphon/en/inv/Chorote.html}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The ELDP archive record above is the only digital collection seen; its audio content was not verified.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools. Material suitable for curriculum work is limited to the dictionary and grammatical descriptions above, and community partnership would be essential.
