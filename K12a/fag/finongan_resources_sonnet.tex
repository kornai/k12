\section{Introduction}
Finongan (ISO 639-3: fag; Glottocode fino1238), also called Finungwa or Finungwan, is a Finisterre-branch Trans-New Guinea language of the Sarawaged Range, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Speaker estimates range from about 1{,}100 to 2{,}600 depending on source and date; Wikitongues rates it vigorous. Villages named include Bakame, Kalange, Gifan and Mama.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Finongan is used by all community members, which favours mother-tongue instruction, but no school use was found. The Webonary dictionary is the most useful starting point for vocabulary and orthography; its coverage and licence should be checked directly.
