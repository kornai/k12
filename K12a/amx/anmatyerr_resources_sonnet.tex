\section{Introduction}
Anmatyerr (also Anmatjirra, Anmatjera; ISO 639-3 amx) is a Pama-Nyungan language of Central Australia, spoken in communities such as Ti Tree, Laramba and Mount Allan, roughly 200 km north of Alice Springs. AIATSIS records a Central and an Eastern dialect. Speaker estimates in the sources found vary (a publisher blurb mentions about 2,000 daily speakers). This report is based on a limited web search and is short on purpose.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
Search results describe the \emph{Central Anmatyerr Picture Dictionary} (Jenny Green, IAD Press, 2003; about 400 illustrations, Anmatyerr and English definitions), which its publisher presents as a resource for school children and teachers. No direct product URL for it appeared in the search results. No school curriculum or primer was found.
