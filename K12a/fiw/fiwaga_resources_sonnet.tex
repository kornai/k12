\section{Introduction}
Fiwaga (ISO 639-3 fiw; also Fimaga, Fiwage) is a Papuan language of Southern Highlands Province, Papua New Guinea, in the East Kutubuan group with Foi. A cited speaker count is 300 (a 1981 source); Joshua Project lists 1,000--1,100 people. It is among the least documented languages of the region.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Documentation is very thin and no orthography or teaching material was found. Any curriculum would first require community-led orthography and materials development, with fieldwork to establish the current status of the language.
