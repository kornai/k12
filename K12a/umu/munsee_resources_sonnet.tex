\section{Introduction}
Munsee (Munsee Delaware; ISO 639-3 umu) is an Eastern Algonquian language, critically endangered. Of the Canadian sites where it was predominant, only Moraviantown, Ontario has surviving speakers. This search found one major dictionary and a community digital dictionary project, but no ready-made school curriculum.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
With about nine speakers, a curriculum would depend on the O'Meara dictionary and close work with the Munsee-Delaware Nation and its language teachers. No school programme was found.
