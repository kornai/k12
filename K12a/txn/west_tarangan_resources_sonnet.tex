\section{Introduction}
West Tarangan (ISO 639-3 txn, Glottocode west2538) is an Austronesian language of the Aru Islands, Indonesia. Searches found only scattered scholarly material and no school materials.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school curricula in West Tarangan were found in search results.
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Wikipedia, Tarangan language (overview and phonology drawn from Nivens): \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarangan_language}
\item Grambank entry for West Tarangan, with a bibliography that lists Nivens 1992 (lexical phonology) and Wattimury et al. 1995, \emph{Sintaksis bahasa Tarangan} (content and availability not verified): \url{https://grambank.clld.org/languages/west2538}
\item WALS reference record, Nivens 1992: \url{https://wals.info/refdb/record/Nivens-1992}
\item Austronesian Comparative Dictionary, West Tarangan word data: \url{https://acd.clld.org/languages/1104}
\item ABVD wordlist, Tarangan Barat (Feruni): \url{https://abvd.eva.mpg.de/austronesian/language.php?id=591}
\end{itemize}
\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found in search results.
\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Endangered Languages Project bibliography for West Tarangan: \url{https://endangeredlanguages.com/lang/10070/bibliography?hl=zh-cn}
\item PARADISEC INIJAK series (Aru and Kei Islands; mostly historical wordlists and articles, not specific to West Tarangan): \url{https://paradisec.org.au/fieldnotes/INIJAK.htm}
\end{itemize}
\section{Curriculum Development Context}
No standard orthography was found, and Dobo Malay and Indonesian are widely used alongside the language. A curriculum would need to begin with new documentation and orthography work.
