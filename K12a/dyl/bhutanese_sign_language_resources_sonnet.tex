\section{Introduction}
Bhutanese Sign Language (BhSL, ISO 639-3 dyl) is the sign language of Bhutan's Deaf community. Its development began with the Deaf Education Unit, established in 2003, now the Wangsel Institute for the Deaf in Paro.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Wangsel teaches using English alongside BhSL. In July 2025 its faculty were reported to have been trained at Mahidol University's Ratchasuda Institute in sign language curriculum design for kindergarten through secondary (\url{https://www.rama.mahidol.ac.th/international/news/event/18072025-1324}). Reports note a lack of interpreters and support after Class XII (\url{https://asianews.network/bhutanese-students-with-disabilities-struggle-for-access-to-higher-education/}).
