\section{Introduction}
Alutor (ISO 639-3 \texttt{alr}) is a Chukotko-Kamchatkan language of the eastern Kamchatka Peninsula (Koryak district), Russia, spoken mainly by the elderly. Search results show only academic documentation; no school material was found. The contents of the pages below were not opened, only seen in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Alutor is not known to be taught in schools, and with about 99 speakers, mostly elderly, any curriculum would depend on the Kibrik grammar and Nagayama dictionaries and on urgent documentation work with remaining speakers. Moll's 1960 Koryak--Russian dictionary concerns the related Koryak language and is not an Alutor resource.
