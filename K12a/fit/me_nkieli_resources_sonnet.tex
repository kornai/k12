\section{Introduction}
Me\"ankieli (Tornedalian Finnish, ISO 639-3 fit, Glottocode torn1244) is a Uralic minority language of northern Sweden, also spoken in Finland. Search results indicate that the number of pupils learning it in Sweden has grown over the last decade, and that the Swedish National Agency for Education (Skolverket) has been developing subject teaching materials, while a shortage of qualified teachers remains. This report lists only resources that appeared in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Sveriges Radio report on Skolverket lesson plans and teaching materials for minority languages including Me\"ankieli (English): \url{https://www.sverigesradio.se/artikel/6133178}
\item Umea University course syllabus (1fi060), whose reading lists in this family of syllabi include Me\"ankieli textbooks such as \emph{Tule sieki} (grades 6--9, Erkheikki and Redebo, 2024) and Pohjanen and Muli, \emph{Me\"ankieli r\"att och l\"att}; the search summary did not give a direct publisher link for the textbooks themselves: \url{https://www.umu.se/en/student/syllabus/1fi060/}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Me\"ankieli is a recognised minority language in Sweden with growing school uptake and ongoing national materials development. The identification text supplied states it is not known to be taught in schools; the search results suggest otherwise for Sweden, so this should be verified. Further searching for current school textbooks, teacher training and audio would be needed.
