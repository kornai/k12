\section{Introduction}
North Mesopotamian Arabic (ISO 639-3 ayp), also called Moslawi or Mardelli, is a variety of Mesopotamian Arabic spoken in Iraq, Syria and Turkey. It belongs to the \emph{qeltu} group of dialects, as distinguished from the \emph{gelet} group. This survey found mainly scholarly descriptions; no school materials in the dialect were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes in North Mesopotamian Arabic were found in the searches. Schooling in the region uses Standard Arabic (or Turkish in Turkey).

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Materials are scholarly and mostly in French, German and Romanian. A curriculum would need to be built from dialect descriptions and new community-based material, alongside Standard Arabic.
