\section{Introduction}
Northern Pwo (Phlong; ISO 639-3 pww) is a Karen language of northwestern Thailand (Chiang Mai, Mae Hong Son, Lamphun, Tak), with about 60,000 speakers according to a 1983 figure cited by Wikipedia and Omniglot. Dialects Mae Ping, Mae Sariang and Omkoi are mutually intelligible. It is written with Thai-script-based orthographies: a 1970s missionary system, a non-formal education orthography of 2003--2006 used in Chiang Mai education projects, and a 2007 orthography combining earlier ones.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools. Several competing Thai-script orthographies exist, so a first curriculum step would be community agreement on one.
