\section{Introduction}
Ayi (ISO 639-3 ayq; Glottolog ayip1235) is a Sepik (Tama) language of southeastern Sandaun Province, Papua New Guinea, spoken in three villages between the Sepik River and Nuku. Speaker counts differ: 430 in the 2000 census, 590 per Global Recordings Network. Alternate names include Besi and Warasai. Very little material exists.
