\section{Introduction}
Binukid (ISO 639-3 bkd; also called Bukid) is an Austronesian language of Northern Mindanao, Philippines. Search results report about 168,000 speakers in the 2010 census and treat the Malaybalay variety as the prestige standard. Scholarly studies are described as scarce. No school materials or dictionary were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
