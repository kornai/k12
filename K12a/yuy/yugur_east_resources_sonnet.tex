\section{Introduction}
Eastern Yugur (ISO 639-3 yuy) is a Mongolic language of Gansu, China, with about 4,000 native speakers (2007). It is unwritten and severely endangered. It shares the Yugur nationality with Western Yugur, which is Turkic and a different language. Its place within Mongolic is still debated. Search results gave general descriptions and almost no teaching materials.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is unwritten and no school instruction was found, so any curriculum would first need a practical orthography and further documentation. The materials found are descriptive rather than pedagogical. A community-led effort and further searches in Chinese-language sources would be needed.
