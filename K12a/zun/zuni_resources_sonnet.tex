\section{Introduction}
Zuni (Shiwi'ma, ISO 639-3 zun; Glottocode zuni1245) is a language isolate of the Pueblo of Zuni in New Mexico, USA. It is taught as a subject in some schools. This report lists only what appeared in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Zuni Public School District Zuni--English dictionary, first published in 1998 with revisions in 2008 and 2009. It is catalogued as an open-access digital book. \url{https://wiki.ubc.ca/Documentation:RelLex/Zuni_Glossary}
\item The A:shiwi College and Career Readiness Center received a state grant for culturally grounded Zuni language education (KUNM, 2019). The instructor is developing a curriculum with former district bilingual educators. \url{https://www.kunm.org/public-health-new-mexico/2019-11-27/zuni-college-gets-state-funds-for-community-based-language-education}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A federal grant document mentions a Din\'e Bizaad \& A:shiwi Language Program at Albuquerque Public Schools, which supports both Navajo and Zuni instruction. \url{https://www.heinrich.senate.gov/download/native-american-language-grant} The searches did not confirm the current state of the Zuni Public School District language programme, or whether the district dictionary is still in print.
