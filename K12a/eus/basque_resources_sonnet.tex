\section{Introduction}
Basque (Euskara, ISO 639-3 eus) is a language isolate, official in the Basque Autonomous Community and parts of Navarre, and used as a language of instruction. The Basque Country and Navarre run bilingual schooling in models A, B and D by degree of Basque-medium teaching, and the northern Basque Country offers immersion and bilingual tracks. This list is limited to what the searches surfaced.
