\section{Introduction}
Sempan is spoken by the Sempan people in Mimika Regency, Central Papua, Indonesia. It is classified in the Asmat--Kamoro group. Speaker numbers of about 1,000 are cited from 1975 and 1987 figures. The search found very little for Sempan. It is described in sources as attested essentially through the work of the missionary linguist Drabbe. This report is therefore short.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None were found. No primer, textbook or school programme for Sempan appeared in the search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little documentation exists, and no teaching materials were found. Curriculum work would have to start from new documentation with the community, for example collecting vocabulary and texts. Drabbe's original publications, which the sources above mention, would be a starting point, but they were not found online in this search. A nearby, related Asmat language is a different language and should not be substituted.
