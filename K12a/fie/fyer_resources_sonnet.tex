\section{Introduction}
Fyer (Fier; ISO 639-3 fie; Glottocode fyer1241) is a West Chadic (Bole--Angas, Ron) language of the Afro-Asiatic family, spoken in Plateau State, Nigeria. About 26,000 speakers are reported (2000 data); the ethnic group is put at about 35,000. Many speakers also use Hausa, and some English or Ngas.
