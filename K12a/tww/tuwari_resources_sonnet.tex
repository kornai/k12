\section{Introduction}
Tuwari (ISO 639-3: tww; Glottocode tuwa1242) belongs to the small Walio family (Walio, Pei, Yawiyo, Tuwari), spoken in the upper Walio River area of Papua New Guinea. Sources give about 330--360 speakers. Glottolog lists it as severely endangered. Sources disagree on the province (Sandaun or East Sepik) and on whether the Walio family is part of Sepik. Very little educational material was found in search results: no primers, textbooks, dictionaries or scripture were located.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Documentation is minimal. Any curriculum would need new fieldwork with the community to produce an orthography, word lists and texts before teaching materials can be built. The reference items above provide only background and classification.
