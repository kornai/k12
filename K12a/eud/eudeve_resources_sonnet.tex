\section{Introduction}
Eudeve (ISO 639-3 eud; also Heve) is an extinct Southern Uto-Aztecan (Taracahitic) language of northern Sonora, Mexico, extinct since about the 1930s. It is closely related to Opata, and some scholars treat the two as dialects. It is known mainly from colonial-era manuscripts. This document therefore supports heritage and revitalization work, not a living-language curriculum.
