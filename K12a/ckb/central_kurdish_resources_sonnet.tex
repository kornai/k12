\section{Introduction}
Central Kurdish (Sorani, ISO 639-3 ckb) is an official language of Iraq and the main language of instruction in the Kurdistan Region. This dossier lists what web search surfaced; it is short and does not claim to be complete.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
The Kurdistan Region Ministry of Education has recently revised school textbooks in several languages including Sorani Kurdish, among them the Grade 1 Kurdish alphabet and Grade 6 Kurdish language books. Press reports give 110 revised textbooks in one round and 142 in an earlier one. I did not find titles or download pages.
\begin{itemize}
\item Kurdistan24 report on the 110-textbook revision: \url{https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/928287/krg-revises-110-textbooks-in-largest-curriculum-reform-to-date}
\item Channel8 report on the textbook overhaul: \url{https://channel8.com/english/news/62829}
\item People in Need on Kurdish-Sorani classes for Syrian refugee students in northern Iraq: \url{https://peopleinneed.net/empowering-syrian-refugee-students-with-kurdish-language-classes-in-northern-iraq-11124gp}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Sorani is the language of instruction in the Kurdistan Region, with a ministry-run textbook system covering kindergarten through Grade 12. Substantial curriculum material exists but was not locatable by search; the Ministry of Education should be contacted directly for the textbook catalogue.
