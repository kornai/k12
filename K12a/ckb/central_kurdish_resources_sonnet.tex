\section{Introduction}
Central Kurdish (Sorani, ISO 639-3 ckb) is an Indo-European language of the Kurdish macrolanguage, an official language of Iraq and used as a language of instruction. This report is based on a single round of web searches and is therefore short. It lists only items that appeared in search results; no school curricula, ministry textbooks or native-language primary materials were found in these searches, and none are claimed here.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No official school textbooks for K-12 were found in the search results. The only course-type material found is:
\begin{itemize}
\item Udemy online course ``Mastering Central Kurdish: a comprehensive guide to Sorani'' (alphabet, pronunciation, basic grammar; a commercial adult-learner course): \url{https://www.udemy.com/course/mastering-central-kurdish-a-comprehensive-guide-to-sorani/}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Kurdish Grammar (Central Kurdish/Soran\^i), Feryad Fazil Omar, Institut f\"ur Kurdische Studien, written in German with detailed verb conjugation tables: \url{https://ifkurds.de/en/component/k2/item/106-kurdish-grammar.html}
\item Review of Murat Baran, \emph{Kurdish Grammar: SORANI Reference Book} (a quick-reference work, not a course; Latin and Kurdish-script editions): \url{https://kurdishcentral.org/baran-kurdish-grammar/}
\item Kurdish-Sorani English/English-Kurdish Sorani Dictionary and Phrasebook (Hippocrene, listing page): \url{https://www.ingramacademic.com/9780781812450/kurdish-sorani-englishenglish-kurdish-sorani-dictionary-and-phrasebook}
\item Swarthmore linguistics course wiki page on Central Kurdish, listing further grammar and course books: \url{https://wikis.swarthmore.edu/ling073/Central_Kurdish}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Sorani is used as a language of instruction in Iraq (Kurdistan Region), but the searches returned no curriculum documents. Developers should seek Kurdistan Regional Government Ministry of Education textbooks directly; this has not been verified here. The materials above are mostly learner-oriented and reference works rather than school materials.
