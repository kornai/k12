\section{Introduction}
Faifi (Faifa, Fayfi; ISO 639-3 fif; Glottocode faif1234) is spoken around the Fifa mountains of Jizan province, southwestern Saudi Arabia. Its status is debated: some treat it as an unusual Arabic dialect, others as a possible descendant of Ancient South Arabian. Wikipedia gives about 50,000 speakers; younger speakers are shifting to other varieties.
