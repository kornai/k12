\section{Introduction}
Feroge (also Ferroge, Feroghe, Kaligi; ISO 639-3 fer) is spoken in Western Bahr el Ghazal state, South Sudan. Sources found describe it as unwritten and shifting, with Arabic widely used alongside it. Little exists beyond encyclopedic and classification pages; no teaching materials were found.
