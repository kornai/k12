\section{Introduction}
Gowro (Gabaro, Gabar Khel, ISO 639-3 gwf) is a small Indo-Aryan (Dardic/Kohistani) language of Kohistan, Pakistan, centred on the village of Mahrin, where Shina is the majority language. Speaker estimates in the sources range from about 200 to about 1,000. Search results show only catalogue-style entries; no teaching materials, grammar, dictionary or recordings were found.
