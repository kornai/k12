\section{Introduction}
Murui Witoto (ISO 639-3 huu) is an endangered Witotoan language of Colombia and Peru. Searches found a small body of academic description and no school materials in the language. Little else was found, and nothing here should be read as evidence of existing school curricula.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in the search results.
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Wikipedia, Murui Huitoto language (overview; mentions Burtch's 1983 Murui--Spanish dictionary and earlier grammatical studies): \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murui_Huitoto_language}
\item Wojtylak, K. I., A grammar of Murui (Bue): a Witotoan language of Northwest Amazonia (JCU thesis record): \url{https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/51983/}

\end{itemize}
\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found in the search results.
\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
None found in the search results; the grammar is based on recorded texts but no public audio collection was located.
\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Only descriptive linguistic work was found. Curriculum building would need to start from the grammar and the dictionary mentioned above, with community partners; those texts were not seen in full text.
