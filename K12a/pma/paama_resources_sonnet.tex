\section{Introduction}
Paama, also called Paamese (ISO 639-3 \texttt{pma}, Glottocode paam1238), is an Austronesian language of Paama island, Vanuatu. About 6,000 speakers are reported. The main documentation is the work of Terry Crowley, both of whose books are openly hosted by the Australian National University (ANU).

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Paamese is described as stable, with all community members speaking it, but it is not known to be taught in schools. The grammar and dictionary give a solid base for developing a vernacular primer and word lists. No Paamese-language teaching materials or local education bodies were identified in the search results.
