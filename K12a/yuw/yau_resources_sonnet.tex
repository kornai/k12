\section{Introduction}
Yau (ISO 639-3 yuw; Glottocode yaum1237) is a Finisterre-Huon (Nuclear Trans New Guinea) language of Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. It must not be confused with the unrelated Torricelli language Yau (ISO yyu) of Sandaun Province. Sources disagree on how Yau relates to Nungon and Uruwa: some treat Yau as the dialect group of the northeastern villages, with Nungon as the southeastern dialects. Little material specific to Yau was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is reported as stable and spoken by about 1,700 people, but no school use was found. Any curriculum would probably start from Nungon documentation, notably Hannah Sarvasy's grammar. That route needs checking with the community, since the dialect relationship is unclear.
