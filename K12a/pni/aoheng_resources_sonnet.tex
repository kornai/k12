\section{Introduction}
Aoheng (Penihing, ISO 639-3 pni) is a Kayan-group Austronesian language of East Kalimantan, Indonesia, spoken along the upper Mahakam around Long Apari and Tiong Bu'u. The figure of about 2,600 speakers dates from 1981 and may be outdated. The searches found almost no educational material; what exists is academic.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is still a first language for all adults but not all young people, and is not known to be taught in schools. Any curriculum would need new documentation work with the community. The Sellato material on traditional measurement and the Blust--Smith bibliography are possible starting points for locating further sources.
