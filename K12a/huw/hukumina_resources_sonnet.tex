\section{Introduction}
Hukumina (ISO 639-3 huw) was an Austronesian language of northwest Buru, Maluku, Indonesia. Search results indicate it is extinct, with one speaker recorded in 1989. Very little documentation exists.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
With no known speakers and only a short word list and a sociolinguistic study, a language-medium curriculum is not feasible. A heritage curriculum could cover the history of the Hukumina people and the Buru language ecology, using the sources above, ideally with Buru community input.
