\section{Introduction}
Katkari (Kathodi; ISO 639-3 kfu; Glottocode katk1238) is an Indo-Aryan language related to Marathi, spoken mainly in Maharashtra. Wikipedia gives an older estimate of 12,000 speakers (2007) and describes it as endangered. No grammar or published dictionary was found.
