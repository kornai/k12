\section{Introduction}
Kadar (ISO 639-3 kej) is a Dravidian language of Kerala and Tamil Nadu, close to Malayalam, with speakers shifting to Tamil and Malayalam. It should not be confused with Cholanaikkan, a different language. Search results gave only reference pages and bibliographic pointers.
