\section{Introduction}
North Fali (ISO 639-3 fll) is an endangered language of northern Cameroon with roughly 16,000 speakers. Reported varieties include Dourbeye, Bossum and B\textschwa ri. Web searches found very little material usable in schools; no primers, textbooks or orthography guides were found. This dossier lists only what appeared in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found. No North Fali primers, readers or textbooks appeared in search results, and the language is not known to be taught in schools.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Kramer, Raija (2014), \emph{Die Sprache der Fali in Nordkamerun} (German-language grammar with a phonological outline covering syllable structure and tone; publisher page): \url{https://www.koeppe.de/titel_die-sprache-der-fali-in-nordkamerun}
\item Ennulat (1971), \emph{Outline of the grammar of Fali}, listed in the SIL Cameroon archive; the listing says it is not available online: \url{https://www.silcam.org/resources/archives/64940}
\item Overview article on the Fali languages of Cameroon: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fali_languages_(Cameroon)}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little documentation exists, so a curriculum would first need community-led orthography and materials development, starting from the grammar and phonology above. The sources here do not show any existing school programme or standardised orthography. Fieldwork, and contact with SIL Cameroon and local speakers, would be needed.
