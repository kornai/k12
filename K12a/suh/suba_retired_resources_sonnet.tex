\section{Introduction}
The code suh (Glottocode suba1254, listed as ``Suba (Retired)'') could not be tied to a living language. ISO 639-3 change request 2007-031 created suh together with ssc (Suba-Simbiti) and sxb (Suba) in 2008. The searches did not show what status suh has now, and they found no Glottolog record for suba1254. The ``Bookkeeping'' family label is a Glottolog placeholder, and the sources give no reason to link it to a particular language. The only well-documented Suba is the Bantu language of Kenya (sxb), covered in the resources below. It is not confirmed to be what suh refers to.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found for suh. For Kenyan Suba (sxb), the Wikipedia article reports that the Kenyan government introduced Suba as a primary school subject in the 1990s. The search turned up no textbooks.
