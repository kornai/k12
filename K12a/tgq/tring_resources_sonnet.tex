\section{Introduction}
Tring (ISO 639-3 tgq; Glottocode trin1273) is an Austronesian language of Borneo spoken in Sarawak, Malaysia. Sources give very different speaker numbers: Wikipedia cites 550 native speakers (2000), and Joshua Project gives about 2,100 people. It should not be confused with Tringgus (trx), a separate Bidayuh language.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
