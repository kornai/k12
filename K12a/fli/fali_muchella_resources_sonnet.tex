\section{Introduction}
Fali Muchella (Madzarin, ISO 639-3 fli) is a variety of Fali of Mubi, spoken in Adamawa State, Nigeria. It is not the Fali language of northern Cameroon. Very little was found: the search returned only general descriptions and a recordings page.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found. Joshua Project reports no significant existing literature in Fali.
