\section{Introduction}
Kven Finnish (ISO 639-3 fkv) is a Uralic language of northern Norway. This dossier is based on a small number of web searches. They turned up policy documents and organisational pages, but no catalogue of specific Kven textbooks, primers or dictionaries. Little verifiable curriculum material was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No specific textbook or primer was identified in the search results. The Council of Europe monitoring documents report that teaching materials, including digital ones, remain insufficient and that teacher recruitment for Kven is weak:
\begin{itemize}
\item Council of Europe news item on Norway and minority languages: \url{https://www.coe.int/en/web/european-charter-regional-or-minority-languages/news/-/asset_publisher/t9sWxmY5eZkv/content/norway-further-improvement-needed-to-revitalise-and-develop-the-most-endangered-minority-languages}
\item Charter evaluation report on Norway: \url{https://rm.coe.int/norwayiria8-en/1680a9b734}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The sources indicate that Kven is recognised under the European Charter and that materials for every level of education still need to be developed. Anyone building a curriculum should contact the Kven organisations and the Norwegian Directorate for Education and Training directly.
