\section{Introduction}
Aore (ISO 639-3 aor; Glottocode aore1237) is an Oceanic (Austronesian) language of Aore Island, off Espiritu Santo, Vanuatu. It is generally classed as extinct (Wikipedia cites the 1980s and UNESCO's extinct classification); the Endangered Languages Project labels it dormant. A source found in search says Tryon (1972) reported a single elderly speaker. No teaching materials were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
There are no known speakers and no found documentation beyond catalogue entries, so a heritage curriculum would have to be built around the history and place of Aore Island and the neighbouring Espiritu Santo languages, with local Vanuatu cultural authorities. I did not find any such programme.
