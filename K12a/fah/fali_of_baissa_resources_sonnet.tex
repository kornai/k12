\section{Introduction}
Fali of Baissa (ISO 639-3: fah; Glottocode bais1242) is an unclassified Benue-Congo language once spoken around Baissa, Taraba State, Nigeria. Wikipedia describes it as extinct; the Endangered Languages Project, citing Roger Blench's Atlas of Nigerian Languages (2012), reports that a few individuals may still speak it on the Falinga Plateau, with low certainty. No current speaker count was found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Documentation appears minimal, so a heritage or revitalization curriculum would first depend on fieldwork with any remaining speakers or descendants on the Falinga Plateau and on locating the sources in the ELP bibliography. Claims about the language's status should be treated as uncertain.
