\section{Introduction}
Aweer (also Aweera, Boni; ISO 639-3 \texttt{bob}, Glottocode awee1242) is a Cushitic language of eastern Kenya, written in the Latin alphabet. Search results report about 7,600 speakers (2009 census) and note that ``Aweer'' is the preferred name because ``Boni'' may be pejorative. Some speakers have shifted to Orma, Somali or Swahili. Only scholarly and background sources were found.
