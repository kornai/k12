\section{Introduction}
Batui (ISO 639-3 zbt) is an Austronesian language of the eastern peninsula of Sulawesi, Indonesia. Glottolog places it in the Saluanic branch of the Saluan--Banggai group of Celebic. Speaker estimates conflict: Wikipedia cites 2,900 (2007), a SIL-based word list about 850, and Joshua Project gives 3,800 for the Batui people. Joshua Project notes that younger people are shifting to Indonesian. Very little material exists, and I found no school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found. The language is not known to be taught in schools.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Batui is used as a first language by adults only, and there is no known schooling in it. A curriculum would have to start from documentation and community work. Indonesian would remain the language of instruction, and Batui could be introduced as a local-content subject once word lists, texts and recordings exist. The related Banggai language has a Wikipedia article, but I did not examine it for materials.
