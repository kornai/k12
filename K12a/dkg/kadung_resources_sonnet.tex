\section{Introduction}
Kadung (ISO 639-3 dkg; Glottocode vagh1248) is a Plateau (Benue-Congo) language of Plateau and Bauchi states, Nigeria. Sources treat it as part of a small dialect cluster with Ya and Bijim (Kwanka); mutual intelligibility with them is reported to be fairly low. Speaker estimates range from about 19,000 to 21,000. Very little educational material was found in search; this report lists only what appeared in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language appears to be written only minimally, with scripture portions as the main published material. A curriculum would need new orthography, primer and reader development in collaboration with the community. A survey of Bijim that also covers Kadung (Decker et al. 2021) was mentioned in a search summary but no URL was seen, so it is not listed as a resource.
