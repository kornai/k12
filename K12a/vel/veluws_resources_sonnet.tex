\section{Introduction}
Veluws is a group of dialects of the Veluwe region in the Netherlands, with West-Veluws and Oost-Veluws as main varieties. Sources disagree on classification (Low Franconian versus Low Saxon). There is no standard Veluws spelling. Little school material was found.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No school textbooks or primers were found.
