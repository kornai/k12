\section{Introduction}
Shamang (ISO 639-3 xsh; alternate names Samang and Samban) is a poorly known Plateau language of Nigeria. Wikipedia lists it in the Hyamic group of Central Plateau languages, in Kaduna State (Kachia and Jema'a areas). It should not be confused with Sha (ISO 639-3 scw), a different, Afro-Asiatic language of Plateau and Kaduna States. The search found very little on Shamang, so this report is short.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Documentation is thin, and the search found no teaching materials in or about Shamang. Work would begin with fieldwork and community consultation. Nigerian school instruction in the area likely relies on Hausa and English, but no source in the search confirmed this. Do not substitute Sha (scw) materials.
