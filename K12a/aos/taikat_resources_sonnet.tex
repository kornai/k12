\section{Introduction}
Taikat (ISO 639-3 aos; Glottocode taik1255; also Daiget, Tajkat, Arso, Abrap) is a Border-family Papuan language spoken around Arso in Keerom Regency, Papua, Indonesia. Search results give conflicting speaker figures (about 500 around 2000, 600 in 1978, a community estimate of about 3,500), and note shift among younger people. No school materials, primers or curricula were found.
