\section{Introduction}
Dhuwal (ISO 639-3 dwu) is an Australian Aboriginal language of north-east Arnhem Land, part of the Yolngu Matha group. Searches found little material labelled specifically as Dhuwal; most resources cover Yolngu Matha broadly or other varieties (for example Djambarrpuyngu). Nothing found shows Dhuwal-specific school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No Dhuwal-specific primers or textbooks were found. One related item is a self-published illustrated children's dictionary in Djambarrpuyngu, a different variety, reported as used at Milingimbi school: Grace Tongatua, \url{https://leanpub.com/u/balandamiyalk}.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The reference description says Dhuwal is not known to be taught in schools. Related bilingual programmes exist in Yolngu communities, but their relation to Dhuwal was not established here. A curriculum would need to build on the dictionary and text material above and on work with Dhuwal speakers.
