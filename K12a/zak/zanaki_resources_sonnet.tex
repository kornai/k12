\section{Introduction}
Zanaki (ISO 639-3 zak, Glottocode zana1238; Ikizanaki, Kizanaki) is a Bantu language of the Atlantic-Congo family, spoken in the Mara Region around Musoma, Tanzania. About 100,000 speakers are estimated. It is reported to have been the first language of Julius Nyerere. The reference description says it is not known to be taught in schools.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Zanaki is spoken by all members of its community, which makes it a candidate for mother-tongue education. The published written material is limited to the New Testament and a few scripture portions. A curriculum would need secular readers, a dictionary and a grammar. The openly licensed New Testament text could serve as a corpus for orthography work. Swahili is the language of schooling in Tanzania.
