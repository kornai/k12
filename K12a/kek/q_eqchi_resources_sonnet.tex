\section{Introduction}
Q'eqchi' (ISO 639-3 kek; Mayan) is spoken in Guatemala and Belize. This report lists only what a limited web search turned up; it is short because the search found few educational resources.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primer or textbook was found in the search results. One overview mentioned that workbooks for rural Guatemalan schools exist as a category, but no specific title or URL was seen, so none is listed.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item A bilingual (Q'eqchi', English, Spanish) phrasebook and travel dictionary offered by a bookseller: \url{https://buxtonvillagebooks.com/book/9781731109422}
\item Omniglot page on Q'eqchi' writing: \url{https://omniglot.com/writing/qeqchi.htm}
\item General encyclopedic overview: \url{https://wikipedia.com/wiki/ISO_639:kek}
\end{itemize}
The search noted two orthographies: an older SIL-derived one used in the Bible translation, and the newer standard from the Proyecto Ling\"u\'istico Francisco Marroqu\'in. Check which one any resource uses.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Audio and app versions of the Bible are available via the ScriptureEarth pages above. No other digital or audio resources were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Only scripture and a phrasebook were found; no grammar, school curriculum or policy documents appeared in this search. Further targeted searching (e.g.\ for Guatemalan bilingual education materials) is needed.
