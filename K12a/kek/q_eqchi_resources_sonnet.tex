\section{Introduction}
Q'eqchi' (ISO 639-3 kek; Glottocode kekc1242) is a Mayan language of Guatemala and Belize with roughly 800,000 speakers. This report lists only resources that appeared in web search results. Search results were mostly bookseller pages, mirrors and scripture sites; no current catalogue of Q'eqchi' school textbooks was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No Q'eqchi' primers or textbooks were found directly in search results. One Wikipedia mirror mentions workbooks designed for rural Guatemalan schools, and search results mention the workbook \emph{Aprendamos Kekchi} (SIL orthography), but no URL for either was found.
\begin{itemize}
\item Mayan languages wiki entry on Q'eqchi': \url{https://mayanlanguages.wiki/node/9906}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Q'eqchi' is a vital language with a large speaker base, but searches found little school material in the language itself. A curriculum would need to build on the ALMG orthography, the Frazier dictionary and scripture texts as sources of vocabulary, and would likely require new content development with community partners. Licensing (for example CC BY-NC-ND for the New Testament) limits reuse.
