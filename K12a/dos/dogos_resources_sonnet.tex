\section{Introduction}
Dogos\'e (ISO 639-3 \texttt{dos}, also spelled Doghose) is a Gur language of Burkina Faso with about 20,000 speakers (1991 figure). It should not be confused with Dogoso (\texttt{dgs}), a separate language of Burkina Faso. The search found only encyclopedic and mission-profile pages, with no teaching materials.
