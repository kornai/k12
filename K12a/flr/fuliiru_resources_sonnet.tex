\section{Introduction}
Fuliiru (Kifuliiru, flr) is a Bantu language of South Kivu province, Democratic Republic of the Congo. Estimates of speakers vary; one 1999 estimate gives 300,000. Search results turned up Scripture and religious media but no dictionary, primer or school material.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found. No literacy programmes, primers or textbooks appeared in search results.

\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The complete Bible text gives a corpus of written Fuliiru, usable for building reading materials. Basic educational resources are missing: orthography documentation, a dictionary, graded readers and teacher guides would all have to be produced. The language is not known to be taught in schools.
