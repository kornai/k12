\section{Introduction}
Yugambal (also Yugumbal, Jukambal; ISO 639-3: yub; Glottocode yuga1244; AIATSIS E11) is an extinct Pama-Nyungan language of northern New South Wales. Its name comes from \emph{juka}, ``no''. Its classification is uncertain; Sharpe (2005) suggests it may have been a dialect of the Yugambeh--Bundjalung chain. Documentation is mostly historical, including unpublished notebooks by John Macpherson and others. Yugambal should not be confused with the related Yugambeh language of South-East Queensland (AIATSIS E17), which is being revived. Yugambeh resources are listed separately below and are not Yugambal materials.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Only historical records of Yugambal exist, so a revitalization curriculum would depend on archival work with AIATSIS and the manuscript collections, led by the relevant Aboriginal communities. The following resources are for the neighbouring Yugambeh language (E17), not Yugambal, and could serve as models or for regional context only:
\begin{itemize}
\item AIATSIS Austlang entry E17, Yugambeh: \url{https://collection.aiatsis.gov.au/austlang/language/e17}
\item State Library of Queensland, \emph{Yugambeh Everyday Words}: \url{https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/Yugambeh%20Everyday%20Words%20revNov2017.pdf}
\item Jellurgal educational resources, including counting and animal-word activities in Yugambeh: \url{https://jellurgal.com.au/services/educational-resources}
\end{itemize}
