\section{Introduction}
Tigrigna (Tigrinya, ISO 639-3: tir) is a Semitic language of Eritrea and Tigray, Ethiopia, written in the Ge'ez script. It is used as a language of instruction. My searches were in English and found no official Ministry of Education textbooks; the findings below are mostly diaspora and learner materials, and searching in Ge'ez script would likely turn up more.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Eritrea reportedly supports education in its nine national languages, and Tigrinya is a language of instruction. A journal article on adult-education materials touches on this context but concerns English materials: \url{https://lesllasp.journals.publicknowledgeproject.org/index.php/lesllasp/article/download/6184/5049/17833}. Official national curricula and textbooks in Tigrinya remain to be located through Ministry of Education sources not reached by these searches.
