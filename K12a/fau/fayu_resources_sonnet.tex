\section{Introduction}
Fayu (also Sehudate; ISO 639-3 \texttt{fau}; Glottocode fayu1238) is a Lakes Plain language of Papua, Indonesia, spoken by about 1,400 people, mainly around Foida, and classed as Vulnerable by UNESCO per the sources found. It is not known to be taught in schools. Only academic descriptive material was found.
