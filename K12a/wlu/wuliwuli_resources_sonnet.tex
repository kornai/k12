\section{Introduction}
Wuliwuli (Wulli Wulli; ISO 639-3 wlu; Glottocode wuli1242) is an extinct Pama-Nyungan language of Queensland, Australia. It was spoken around Eidsvold and the Auburn River catchment, in what is now the North Burnett Regional Council area. It is regarded as closely related to, or a dialect of, Wakka Wakka. The name comes from the pronoun \emph{wuli}, ``they''. The surviving record is fragmentary.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language has no speakers, so a heritage and revitalization curriculum would depend on the old word lists, AIATSIS holdings and the Wakka Wakka materials. It should be developed with the relevant Aboriginal community and language custodians. I found no existing programme.
