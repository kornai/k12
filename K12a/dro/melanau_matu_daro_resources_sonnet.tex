\section{Introduction}
Matu Daro Melanau (ISO 639-3 \texttt{dro}; Glottocode daro1239) is an Austronesian language of the Melanau group in Sarawak, Malaysia. Speaker counts in sources conflict (7,600 from a 1981 citation; Joshua Project lists 23,000 people).
