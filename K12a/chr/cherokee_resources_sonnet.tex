\section{Introduction}
Cherokee (ISO 639-3 chr) is an endangered Iroquoian language of the United States. This report lists only resources that appeared in web search results; it is short and is not a complete curriculum survey. No single published K-12 curriculum was found in the search.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Cherokee Nation page on learning Cherokee, which describes the immersion school (reported as pre-K through eighth grade) and Sequoyah High School immersion opportunities (grades nine through twelve), as well as free online courses (Cherokee 101, 1, 2, 3) and adult immersion classes: \url{https://cherokee.gov/life-events/learning-cherokee-2}
\item Cherokee Nation page ``Starting young'' on early-years Cherokee language learning: \url{https://www.cherokee.gov/life-events/learning-the-cherokee-language/}
\end{itemize}
Sources disagree on the exact grade range of the immersion school; this should be verified with the Cherokee Nation.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item A 2008 CARLA immersion conference language plan that mentions coordinating with local school districts on Cherokee classes at all grade levels (dated): \url{https://archive.carla.umn.edu/conferences/past/immersion2008/documents/Peter_L_LanguagePlan.pdf}
\end{itemize}
No dictionary or grammar was identified in the search results.

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
No religious resources were found in the search results. The Cherokee Nation Language Department is the main community institution identified.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Apple newsroom report (May 2026) on the Cherokee Immersion School and Sequoyah High School receiving iPads and Macs: \url{https://www.apple.com/ph/newsroom/2026/05/cherokee-language-learners-bridge-generations-with-ipad-and-mac/}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Cherokee Nation operates immersion education in Tahlequah, Oklahoma, so curriculum development should be coordinated with its Language Department. The search results did not provide verified details of the published curriculum materials; these should be requested directly.
