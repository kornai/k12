\section{Introduction}
Bannoni (ISO 639-3 \texttt{bcm}; also Banoni, Tsunari) is an Austronesian language of Bougainville, Papua New Guinea, spoken along Empress Augusta Bay. Speaker counts differ (about 1,000 in Wikipedia, 3,800 in Joshua Project citing Ethnologue). It is closely related to Piva. Only documentation-oriented materials were found; no school materials.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The Capell archive at PARADISEC holds Banoni materials, including a vocabulary list and notes on orthography: \url{https://paradisec.org.au/fieldnotes/NSBBAN.htm} and \url{https://catalog.paradisec.org.au/repository/BP3/006}. I did not verify the access conditions.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little published material exists. Any curriculum would build on the Lincoln description, the PARADISEC archival items and community knowledge, with orthography work required. Close relatedness to Piva may allow shared materials.
