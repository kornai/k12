\section{Introduction}
Maasina Fulfulde (Maasinankoore; ISO 639-3 ffm) is the most widely spoken Fula variety in Mali and has national-language status. One search found background and a lexicon but no Malian school textbooks or official curriculum documents.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No Malian school textbooks were found. A report mentions classroom materials and a children's alphabet book for Pulaar (a different Fula variety) in connection with the ADLaM script: \url{https://www.blackenterprise.com/barry-brothers-microsoft-and-mccann-pulaar-fulani/amp/}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Humery (2016), ``La litt\'eratie pulaar,'' discusses mother-tongue literacy programmes in the Senegal River valley (Senegal, not Mali), useful as a comparison: \url{https://journals.openedition.org/cres/2348}. Malian ministry materials for national-language instruction were not found and should be sought directly.
