\section{Introduction}
Standard Moroccan Tamazight (Amazigh) is a standardised language of Morocco, created by IRCAM from Tashelhit, Central Atlas Tamazight and Tarifit. It is written in the Tifinagh-IRCAM script. Search found mainly background and scholarly sources, not current teaching materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
The Leiden dissertation listed below names the ``Tifawin a Tamazight'' textbooks as an early IRCAM initiative. The same sources report that IRCAM supervises Tifinagh textbooks and teaching aids. I did not find the textbooks themselves online.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Moroccan Tamazight has an institutional standard and teaching tradition under IRCAM, but the standard is contested because it conflicts with the practices of the main varieties. The script choice is also disputed. Curriculum work should check IRCAM and the Moroccan Ministry of Education directly for current textbooks, since search did not find them. A 2004 article on its introduction into schools is at \url{https://ezorigin.csmonitor.com/2004/0817/p14s01-legn.html}.
