\section{Introduction}
Swabian (ISO 639-3 swg) is an Upper German dialect group spoken mainly around Stuttgart and in Bavarian Swabia. Searches found dictionaries and some academic material but no school curriculum, primer or textbook in Swabian. This report lists only what appeared in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No Swabian primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in the search results.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Sch\"abisches Handw\"orterbuch (Mohr Siebeck): Swabian--German and German--Swabian dictionary, 4th edition 2012, with an e-book edition reported for 2025. \url{https://www.mohrsiebeck.com/en/book/schwaebisches-handwoerterbuch-9783161520297}
\item Lexilogos Swabian dictionary page: links to a Swabian--English dictionary by Thomas Kemmer and a Swabian--German dictionary with an introduction to the dialect by Peter-Michael Mangold. \url{https://www.lexilogos.com/english/swabian_dictionary.htm}
\item DIBS, the Dialectology Information System for Bavarian Swabia (Bavarian Academy of Sciences): a free online dictionary of the Swabian dialects of Bavarian Swabia. \url{https://badw.de/en/research-hub/current-projects/translate-to-english-dibs}
\item Bryksa, bachelor's thesis (2016): a comparison of Swabian and standard German grammar and phonetics. It draws mostly on memes and a folk song, so it is an overview and not a full grammar. \url{https://ddd.uab.cat/pub/tfg/2016/tfg_45816/TFG_2015-16_FTI_Bryksa.pdf}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Swabian is not known to be taught in schools, and the search results contained no curriculum material. A curriculum would have to be built from the dictionaries above and from new materials. A modern standalone Swabian grammar was not found.
