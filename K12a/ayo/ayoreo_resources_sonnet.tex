\section{Introduction}
Ayoreo (ISO 639-3 ayo) is a Zamucoan language of Paraguay and Bolivia; its sister language is Chamacoco. The search found linguistic work but no school materials and no Bible translation.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found in search results.

The language is not known to be taught in schools. Bertinetto's 2009 grammatical sketch and Ciucci's 2014 linguistic introduction to an Ayoreo anthropological dictionary were mentioned in search summaries, but no URL for them was seen, so they are not cited.
