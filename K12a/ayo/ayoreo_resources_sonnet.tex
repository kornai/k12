\section{Introduction}
Ayoreo (ISO 639-3 ayo) is a Zamucoan language of Paraguay and Bolivia. A bilingual dictionary and grammar were reported in Paraguay in November 2025, aimed at schools.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item News report on the bilingual dictionary for schools: \url{https://www.lanacion.com.py/pais_edicion_impresa/2025/11/13/ayoreos-reciben-diccionario-bilingue-para-las-escuelas/}
\end{itemize}
Ayore.org is reported to host reading primers, but no specific primer URL was seen in the results.
