\section{Introduction}
Sochiapam Chinantec (ISO 639-3 \texttt{cso}) is an Otomanguean language of Oaxaca, Mexico. Documentation is modest: a living dictionary, a published grammar, wordlists and missionary audio.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in search results.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Sochiapam Chinantec Living Dictionary (Living Tongues Institute, Municipalidad de San Pedro Sochiapam, Observatorio de Lenguas Indígenas), with audio: \url{https://livingdictionaries.app/sochiapam-chinantec}
\item ASJP profile and wordlist; it cites Foris (2000), \emph{A grammar of Sochiapan Chinantec}, SIL (the grammar itself was not located online): \url{https://asjp.clld.org/languages/CHINANTEC_SOCHIAPAN}
\item Numerals page, MPI-EVA: \url{https://lingweb.eva.mpg.de/channumerals/Chinantec-Sochiapam.htm}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
Global Recordings Network lists gospel and Bible-teaching recordings: \url{https://www.globalrecordings.net/en/language/cso}

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The Living Dictionary (above) includes audio; Global Recordings (above) provides recordings. Wikitongues language page: \url{https://wikitongues.org/languages/cso/}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is written in Latin script. No evidence of school use was found. The Living Dictionary and Foris's grammar are the natural starting points for orthography and vocabulary work, in partnership with the municipality of San Pedro Sochiapam.
