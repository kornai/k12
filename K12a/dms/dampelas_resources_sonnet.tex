\section{Introduction}
Dampelas (ISO 639-3 \texttt{dms}; also Dian, Dampal, Dampelasa) is an Austronesian language of Central Sulawesi, Indonesia, in the Tomini-Tolitoli group. Estimates of speakers range from about 2,000 to about 9,500. Sources describe it as threatened, with younger people shifting to Indonesian, and report no literacy in it.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools and has no established literacy materials. A curriculum would depend on new orthography and materials development with the community, drawing on the sketch grammar for linguistic background.
