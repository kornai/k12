\section{Introduction}
Manda (ISO 639-3: zma; Glottocode mand1416) is a Western Daly language of the Northern Territory, Australia, also recorded as Manhthe or Menthe. Glottolog, citing Ethnologue, lists it as extinct. The Endangered Languages Project still labels it critically endangered, and ASJP gives extinct since 2000. A summary of Moseley's \emph{Encyclopedia of the World's Endangered Languages} (2007) says that perhaps twenty-five speakers were reported in 1983, all bilingual in Kriol or English. It should not be confused with Marimanindji (zmm), a separate Western Daly language.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Manda is not spoken today, and the search results showed no teaching materials. A heritage or revitalization programme would depend on the surviving documentation, which would have to be located through the sources above and through Australian archives. Those archives were not searched here. Any such work should be led by the descendant community, and no community organisation was identified in the search results.
