\section{Introduction}
Iten (Eten, Aten, Niten, Ganawuri; ISO 639-3 etx; Glottocode eten1239) is a language of Plateau and Kaduna States, Nigeria, with about 40,000 speakers (2003 figure). Search results rate it as vigorous. Very little was found, and nothing designed for schools. It should not be confused with Etung, a Cross River State area whose language material concerns Ejagham.
