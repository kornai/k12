\section{Introduction}
Foia Foia (ISO 639-3 ffi) is a Trans-New Guinea language of Papua New Guinea with a few hundred speakers (180 in the 2000 census per Wikipedia; about 300 per other listings). Sources disagree on its location. Search results show very little documentation and no educational materials.
