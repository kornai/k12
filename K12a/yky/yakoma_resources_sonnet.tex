\section{Introduction}
Yakoma (ISO 639-3: yky; Glottocode yako1252) is an Atlantic-Congo language of the Central African Republic, also spoken in the DR Congo. Speaker estimates differ: Global Recordings Network gives 110,000, and Joshua Project gives 176,000, of whom 152,000 are in the CAR and 24,000 in the DRC. The search found no evidence that it is taught in schools.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The search found only a few resources, and they are religious or descriptive. The language is written and has Bible portions, which gives a starting point for an orthography and text corpus. Dictionaries, grammars and school materials should be sought through the literature or local organisations. Sango is the national lingua franca of the CAR, so a curriculum should account for its role.
