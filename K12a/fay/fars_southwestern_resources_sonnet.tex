\section{Introduction}
Southwestern Fars (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fay}, Glottocode sout2645) is a label for a group of Fars-province varieties. Glottolog lists alternative names including Davani dialect, and its entry notes Lari as definitely endangered. The label is not a single standardised language, so resources are mostly about individual varieties such as Lari. The searches found no school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes in any Southwestern Fars variety were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Because fay covers several distinct, partly mutually unintelligible varieties and the language is thought to be used as a first language by adults only, any curriculum would first have to choose a variety, and would need new materials. Persian is the language of schooling in Iran.
