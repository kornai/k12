\section{Introduction}
Tewe (ISO 639-3: twx; also Chitewe, Chiute, Teve) is a Bantu language closely related to Shona, spoken mainly in Manica and Sofala provinces of central Mozambique. Search results describe it as largely unwritten, with partial Scripture and audio materials. No general dictionary, textbook or school programme was found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers or textbooks in Tewe were found in search results.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
A Brazilian university repository describes a planned bilingual Tewe--Portuguese dictionary of proverbs, based on 149 expressions collected from native speakers. It is a proverb collection, not a general dictionary.
\begin{itemize}
\item Repository record: \url{https://repositorio.ufcat.edu.br/handle/tede/6878}
\item Repository item: \url{https://repositorio.ufcat.edu.br/items/a913fb7d-cdf4-4ce6-be32-0318d17bb48f}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little written material exists. A curriculum would first need an agreed orthography and written texts, and could build on the audio Scripture and the proverb collection. Contact with local Tewe speakers and the organisations above would be necessary; no contact details were found.
