\section{Introduction}
Kirya-Konzel (ISO 639-3 fkk; alternate names Fali of Kirya, Fali of Mijilu, Karya, Konzal) is a Chadic language of Adamawa State, Nigeria, with about 5,800 speakers (2011). Its two varieties, Kirya and Mijilu (Konzel), are very close. Wikitongues rates it EGIDS 6a (vigorous). Hausa is the language of wider communication.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is vigorous in homes and farms, but no written educational material was found; literacy is in Hausa. Curriculum work would need community orthography and materials development.
