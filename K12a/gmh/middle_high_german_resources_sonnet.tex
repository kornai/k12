\section{Introduction}
Middle High German (ISO 639-3 gmh; Glottocode midd1343) is a historical stage of German (roughly 1050--1350) with no native speakers. Resources are mostly university-level. I found no K-12 materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Tübingen ILIAS module on Middle High German grammar, with pronunciation audio and self-tests: \url{https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/handle/10900/46549?show=full}
\item Link collection of external learning resources, TU Dresden: \url{https://tu-dresden.de/gsw/slk/germanistik/altgerm/studium/links/copy_of_index_html}
\item \emph{An Introduction to Middle High German} (Jones and Jones), introductory grammar with graded texts: \url{https://blackwells.co.uk/bookshop/product/An-Introduction-to-Middle-High-German-by-Howard-Jones-Martin-H-Jones/9780198893998}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The Oxford Guide to Middle High German}: \url{https://www.betterworldbooks.com/product/detail/-9780199654611}
\item University of Wisconsin searchable Middle High German text and dictionary database (free login reported): \url{https://search.library.wisc.edu/database/UWI12446}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found in search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Audio pronunciation examples are part of the Tübingen module above.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Material is aimed at university students. A school curriculum would have to adapt these sources, for example as a short reading-based unit within German or medieval history.
