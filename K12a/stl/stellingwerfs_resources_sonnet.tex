\section{Introduction}
Stellingwerfs (Stellingwarfs, ISO 639-3 \texttt{stl}) is a Low Saxon variety spoken in the Stellingwerf region of southeastern Friesland, Netherlands. It was officially recognised in 1996 as part of Low Saxon, and about 5,000 native speakers were reported in 2016. The searches found no evidence that it is taught in schools. The resources below are limited to what the searches returned.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Stellingwerfs turned up. The only educational activity found was a magazine and adult classes set up in the 1970s, reported in a general overview.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Stellingwarfs Woordeboek}, a four-part dictionary published 1994--2004 with more than 3,800 pages. Its standardised spelling goes back to H.J. Bergveld. It is described in a Taal en Tongval report (2005): \url{https://www.journal-archive.aup.nl/taal-en-tongval/jrg_2005_vol_57_-_vdw-berichten.pdf}
\item A concise Stellingwerfs--Dutch dictionary and a Dutch--Stellingwerfs volume, reported as completed or in progress in that same report.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is stable and has a standardised orthography and a large dictionary, but no school materials were found. A curriculum would have to build on the dictionary and the orthography, and the local Frisian and Low Saxon cultural organisations would need to be contacted directly. Little else was found online.
