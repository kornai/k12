\section{Introduction}
Gbesi Gbe (ISO 639-3: gbs; Glottocode: gbes1238) is a Gbe language of southern Benin. Joshua Project places the people mainly in the Atlantique Department (Kpomass\`e, Allada, Tori-Bossito) and lists about 144,000 people. Sources disagree on subgrouping: Wikipedia places it with Ayizo and Kotafon, Joshua Project in an Eastern Gbe cluster. Very little published material was found.
