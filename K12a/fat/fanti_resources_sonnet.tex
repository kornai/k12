\section{Introduction}
Fanti (Fante, ISO 639-3 fat) is a member of the Akan dialect continuum in Ghana, mutually intelligible with Twi. A standardized form is reportedly taught in primary and secondary schools. Search results gave mostly general and historical sources.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No current Fante school textbooks or primers were found. A 1942 Fante word list approved for schools is mentioned in historical sources, with no online copy seen.
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
Christaller's 1881 dictionary of the Asante and Fante language (Tshi) and 1875 grammar are the classic references; they were described in a search summary of Akuapem Twi: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akuapem_Twi}. Overview: \url{https://wikipedia.com/wiki/ISO_639:fat}.
\section{Religious and Community Resources}
Biography of Gaddiel Robert Acquaah, Methodist educator and Fante Bible translator, author of \emph{Fanti Proverbs}: \url{https://dacb.org/stories/ghana/acquaah-gr/}.
\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
No digital or audio resources were found.
\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Fante is taught in Ghanaian schools, but online materials appear scarce. Further search of Ghana Education Service, Bureau of Ghana Languages and Bible Society of Ghana holdings is advised.
