\section{Introduction}
Sunum (ISO 639-3 ymn) is listed on Wikipedia under the name Yamna. It is an Austronesian language of Papua province, Indonesia, spoken on the coast and an island of Jayapura Bay. Wikipedia's infobox gives about 560 speakers, from 2005 data. It belongs to the Sarmi--Jayapura group. This report rests on a single web search, and very little was found.
