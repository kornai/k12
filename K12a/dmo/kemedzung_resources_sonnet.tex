\section{Introduction}
Kemedzung (also Kemezung, Dumbo, Dumbu; ISO 639-3 \texttt{dmo}; Glottocode keme1240) is an Eastern Beboid language of the Northwest Region of Cameroon, spoken in the villages of Dumbu and Kwei in Misaje Subdivision, Donga-Mantung Division. Sources give about 3,450--3,540 speakers. A Kemezung Language Committee developed a Latin-based writing system in 2007 (based on the General Alphabet of Cameroon Languages) and revised it in 2022.

The language has an approved orthography and an online dictionary, which are the basis for any primer work. No evidence of use in schools was found. Contact with the Kemezung Language Committee and SIL Cameroon would be the natural next step; no contact details were found in search results.
