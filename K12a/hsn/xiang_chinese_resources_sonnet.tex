\section{Introduction}
Xiang Chinese (ISO 639-3 hsn) is spoken mainly in Hunan, with tens of millions of speakers. My search found background and scholarly material but no Xiang textbooks, primers or school programmes.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None found in search results.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Yunji Wu, \emph{A Synchronic and Diachronic Study of the Grammar of the Chinese Xiang Dialects} (De Gruyter Mouton, 2011), catalogue record. \url{https://oeaw.ac.at/resources/Record/9783110927481}
\item New Xiang (Wikipedia). \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Xiang}
\item Xiangxiang dialect (Wikipedia). \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiangxiang_dialect}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
None found in search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Omniglot's Xiang page gives an overview and mentions the Pinfa romanization adapted for Xiang. \url{https://www.omniglot.com/chinese/xiang.htm}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
No Xiang-medium teaching materials were found. Any curriculum would need to be built from linguistic descriptions such as the above, in coordination with the standard Chinese school system.
