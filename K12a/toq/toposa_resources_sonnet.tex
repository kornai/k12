\section{Introduction}
Toposa (ISO 639-3 \texttt{toq}; Glottocode topo1242) is an Eastern Nilotic language of South Sudan. Speaker estimates in the search results differ widely: Wikipedia gives roughly 320,000 (2017) and Omniglot about 100,000. The search found little published material.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item A 2021 UNICEF request for proposals covered literacy primers for primary 2 and 3 in five South Sudanese languages. I could not confirm that Toposa is one of them. \url{https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/133550}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
South Sudan's General Education Act 2012 makes the mother tongue the language of instruction in P1--P3, according to a search summary; I did not check the Act itself. No Toposa textbook was found. The Spagnolo grammar and dictionary is a possible starting point, but I could not confirm that it is available.
