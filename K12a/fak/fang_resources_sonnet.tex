\section{Introduction}
Fang (ISO 639-3: fak) is a small Southern Bantoid language of southern Cameroon, with about 4,000 speakers reported for 2011; ``Fang'' is the name of the village where it is spoken. It is distinct from the much larger Bantu Fang (Pahouin) language of Equatorial Guinea and Gabon, and it is related to Bulu and Ewondo. The searches found only encyclopedic coverage and no school materials.
