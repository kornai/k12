\section{Introduction}
Gbaya-Mbodomo (ISO 639-3 gmm; Glottocode gbay1281; also Mbodomo, Bodomo, Mbadomo) is a Gbaya language of eastern Cameroon, also listed for the Central African Republic. Speaker estimates differ between sources (30,000 and 38,000). Documentation found is thin.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found in search results.
