\section{Introduction}
Fipa is a Bantu language of the Ufipa plateau, Rukwa Region, Tanzania, with about 200,000 speakers. Very little was found; no school materials or dictionary appeared in search results.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools. A curriculum would need to start from oral and scripture-derived material and new orthographic work.
