\section{Introduction}
Ezaa (also spelled Ezza or Eza; ISO 639-3 \texttt{eza}) is an Igboid language of Ebonyi State, Nigeria. Search results describe a dialect cluster (Izii, Ikwo, Mgbo) of limited mutual intelligibility, and a close relationship with the separately coded Izi (Izhi) language. Speaker estimates in the sources found conflict (hundreds of thousands to over a million), and all sources found are encyclopedic or student-written, not authoritative. No school materials in Ezaa were found.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little documentation exists in what was found. Any curriculum would likely need primary fieldwork and community partnership, and could consult Igbo and Izi materials only with care, since they are distinct languages.
