\section{Introduction}
Volap\"uk (ISO 639-3 vol) is a constructed language created by Johann Martin Schleyer in 1879. Modern learners generally follow Arie de Jong's reformed version; older reprints follow Schleyer's Volap\"uk Rigik. No school programme was found in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Evertype, \emph{A Quick Look at Volap\"uk} (free pamphlet with grammar and practice sentences): \url{https://volapuk.evertype.com/quick-look-at-volapuk.html}
\item Linderfelt, \emph{Volap\"uk: An Easy Method} (historical textbook, full view): \url{https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/101708893}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item De Jong, \emph{W\"orterbuch der Weltsprache} (Evertype reprint, German-language dictionary): \url{https://evertype.com/books/dejong-worterbuch.html}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Materials exist for adult self-study, but nothing designed for K-12 was found. A school curriculum would need adaptation of the existing grammar and dictionary reprints.
