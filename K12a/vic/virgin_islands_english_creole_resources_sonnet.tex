\section{Introduction}
Virgin Islands English Creole (ISO 639-3 vic) is an English-based creole of the U.S. and British Virgin Islands. This report rests on a single web search, which turned up almost nothing school-oriented. The extinct Dutch-based Negerhollands is a different language and is listed only to keep the two apart.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No textbooks, primers or school programmes for Virgin Islands English Creole were found.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Locals often call the language a ``dialect'', and it is not known to be taught in schools. A curriculum would need new documentation work, probably starting from the St.\ Croix thesis and its bibliography.
