\section{Introduction}
Umbugarla (Mbukarla; ISO 639-3 umr; Glottocode umbu1235) is an extinct, possibly isolate language of Arnhem Land, Northern Territory, Australia. It was reportedly spoken by only three people in 1981. Little survives beyond a student sketch grammar and archived field notes. No school materials were found.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Umbugarla were found in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A revitalization curriculum would have to be reconstructed from the Davies grammar and the Breen word lists. Any use should involve the relevant Arnhem Land communities and AIATSIS. No contacts are given here because none were found.
