\section{Introduction}
Warlpiri (ISO 639-3 wbp) is spoken in the Northern Territory, Australia. Contrary to the premise that it is not known to be taught, the search results show a long-running bilingual programme at Yuendumu and use as a language of instruction at Yuendumu and Nyirrpi schools.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Bilingual education began at Yuendumu in 1975. Yuendumu School aims for bilingual biliteracy, treating Warlpiri as both teaching medium and learning goal. Further school materials would need searching beyond what was found here.
