\section{Introduction}
Kinga (Kikinga, Ekikinga) is a Bantu language of Tanzania, with roughly 140,000 to 150,000 speakers according to the sources found. Search turned up very little educational material. It found no Kinga dictionary or school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes in Kinga were found in search.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Kinga is widely spoken, but written material seems limited to scripture and recordings. A curriculum would need to produce its own primers and reading materials, and could draw on the New Testament text and audio as sample text. A Kinga dictionary and orthography documentation should be sought through local sources, such as the Bible translation organisations and Tanzanian universities.
