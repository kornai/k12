\section{Introduction}
Gbagyi (ISO 639-3: gbr; Glottocode: gbag1258) is a Niger-Congo language of Nigeria with about 700,000 speakers, classed as endangered. Joshua Project reports a larger ethnic population of about 1,472,000 and notes eastern and western groups speaking different languages. Resources found are few.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Gbagyi Boda} (2014) by Donaye Shekwolo, Samtom Creation, a reading and writing primer covering the alphabet, consonant-vowel combinations and topical vocabulary: \url{https://nigeriareposit.nln.gov.ng/items/9932ec80-a564-438d-81d4-5131afd63ed0/full}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A primer and a Bible text exist, which gives a basis for early literacy. The grammar is a 1979 item whose content was not examined. No school programme was found, and a dictionary and graded readers would be needed.
