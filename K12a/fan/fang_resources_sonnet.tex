\section{Introduction}
Fang (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fan}) is a Bantu language spoken in Equatorial Guinea, northern Gabon and southern Cameroon, with smaller communities in the Republic of the Congo and S\~ao Tom\'e and Pr\'incipe. It is related to Bulu and Ewondo. Several orthographies exist across countries, originally developed by missionaries.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Fang is widely spoken but the search found no modern grammar, dictionary or school materials, and the orthographies differ between countries. A curriculum effort would need to settle on a standard orthography and locate current dictionaries and grammars (possibly in Spanish or French) that this search did not surface.
