\section{Introduction}
Maringarr (Marri Ngarr, Marringarr; ISO 639-3 zmt, Glottocode mari1419) is a critically endangered Western Daly language of the northwest coast of the Northern Territory, Australia. The Marri Ngarr community is now centred on Wadeye and its outstations. Most people there have shifted to Murrinh-Patha, Kriol and English. The sources I found give 5 speakers in the 2016 census. Searches found no school materials. The Glottolog identifiers on the pages found differ from the one given in the task, so the identification of these pages with zmt is not fully certain.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools, and only a very few elderly speakers remain. Bicevskis' grammar is the best starting point for a community-led revitalisation effort in the Wadeye area. Little else is available online.
