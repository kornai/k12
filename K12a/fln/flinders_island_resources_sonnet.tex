\section{Introduction}
Flinders Island (fln) is an extinct Pama-Nyungan language of Queensland, Australia, spoken on Flinders Island off the Queensland coast. It is reported as unconfirmed as a distinct language, with 3 speakers reported in 1981. It should not be confused with the Flinders Island lingua franca of the Tasmanian Furneaux Group, which is a different language. No teaching or revitalization material for fln was found.
