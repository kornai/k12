\section{Introduction}
Dakka (ISO 639-3 dkk; Glottocode dakk1238) is an endangered Austronesian language of the Northern South Sulawesi group (Pitu Ulunna Salu), spoken in the Wonomulyo district of Polewali Mandar Regency, West Sulawesi, Indonesia. About 1,000 speakers were reported in 2010. Search found almost nothing beyond encyclopedic and classification entries.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
With a small, ageing speaker base and no known written materials, any curriculum work would begin with documentation and community-led orthography decisions. The classic descriptive source cited by Wikipedia is Friberg and Laskowske (1989) in NUSA 17; no URL for it was seen, so it is not listed.
