\section{Introduction}
Whitesands (also called East Tanna or Narak; ISO 639-3 tnp) is an Oceanic language spoken on the eastern coast of Tanna, Vanuatu. It is closely related to North Tanna and Lenakel. Little published educational material turned up in search. Kwamera (Nafe) is a different language, and its materials are not listed here.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
The PARADISEC collection of Jeremy Hammond's 2008 Whitesands documentation is reported to include a Whitesands--French school word list and storybooks. I did not open the items, so contents and access conditions are unverified.
\begin{itemize}
\item PARADISEC item: \url{https://catalog.paradisec.org.au/repository/JHWS1/20080503}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
No Whitesands dictionary, textbook or curriculum was found. A curriculum would have to start from the Hammond documentation, the PARADISEC materials and work with the community. This dossier is short because little was found.
