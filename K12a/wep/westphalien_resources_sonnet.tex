\section{Introduction}
Westphalian (ISO 639-3 wep) is a major dialect group of Low German (Plattdeutsch) in North Rhine-Westphalia and Lower Saxony, Germany. It includes M\"unsterl\"andisch and Ostwestf\"alisch. Search results turned up some dictionaries and an archive, but no school-oriented materials.
