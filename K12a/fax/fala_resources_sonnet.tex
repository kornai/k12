\section{Introduction}
Fala (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fax}) is a Western Romance language, usually classed with Galician-Portuguese and showing some Leonese traits. It is spoken in three towns of the Jálama valley in northwestern Extremadura, Spain (Valverde del Fresno, Eljas and San Martín de Trevejo). Estimates in the sources found are roughly 10,500 speakers. Local varieties are called valverdeiru, lagarteiru and mañegu. Searches found scholarly work and an adult course, but no school textbooks or primers. This dossier is therefore short.

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
The association Fala i Cultura (founded 1992) is mentioned in the Wikipedia article, with a goal of compiling a common grammar. No community website or publication of that grammar was confirmed in the results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A Fala is protected as a Bien de Inter\'es Cultural (Extremadura decree 45/2001, according to search summaries). The search results did not show whether Fala is part of the regular school curriculum; the Junta de Extremadura education department and the Instituto Val de X\'alima would be the places to ask. Any curriculum would need to begin from the scholarly descriptions above and from new material, since no classroom materials were found.
