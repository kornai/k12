\section{Introduction}
Pulaar is spoken in Senegal, Mauritania and neighbouring countries. Searches found a dictionary, scripture and audio items and research on the Pulaar literacy movement, but no confirmed Pulaar school textbooks and no information on current school use in Mauritania.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No textbooks or primers were found in search.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
Hippocrene Pulaar--English/English--Pulaar standard dictionary (print, about 30,000 entries): \url{https://www.hippocrenebooks.com/store/p275/Pulaar-English_StandardDictionary.html}.

Lexilogos page of Fula/Peul dictionary and linguistic links: \url{https://www.lexilogos.com/peul_dictionnaire.htm}.

Research on the Pulaar movement and mother-tongue literacy (Humery): \url{https://journals.openedition.org/cres/2348}.

Research page on the Pulaar book network (SOAS): \url{https://mulosige.soas.ac.uk/tag/west-africa/}.

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
Scripture Earth listing for Pulaar: \url{https://www.scriptureearth.org/00eng.php?idx=744}.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Global Recordings Network, Pulaar: \url{https://www.globalrecordings.net/en/language/fuc}.

MegaVoice children's audio Bible in Pulaar: \url{https://megavoice.com/media-cloud/m014122-bible-for-children-fuc-pulaar-audio-bible}.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Pulaar has an active literacy movement, especially around Fuuta Tooro, but the searches did not surface school materials; these should be sought from national literacy and education authorities and local organisations.
