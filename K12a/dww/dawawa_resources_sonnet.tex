\section{Introduction}
Dawawa (ISO 639-3 dww; Glottocode dawa1242) is an Austronesian language of Milne Bay Province, Papua New Guinea, spoken around Cape Vogel by roughly 2,000--2,500 people. Little beyond a Bible translation was found in search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Dawawa is reported as a first language of its community and not known to be taught in schools. The scripture text is the only substantial written material found, so a curriculum would need new materials developed with the community.
