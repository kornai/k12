\section{Introduction}
Adamorobe Sign Language (AdaSL, ISO 639-3 ads) is a village sign language of Adamorobe, an Akan village in eastern Ghana. Both deaf and hearing residents use it. It is generally considered an isolate, unrelated to Ghanaian Sign Language, and it is endangered. Deaf children attend a boarding school where Ghanaian Sign Language is the language of instruction.
