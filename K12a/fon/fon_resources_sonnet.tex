\section{Introduction}
Fon (Fongbe; ISO 639-3 \texttt{fon}) is a Gbe language of Benin and Togo with roughly 1.9 million speakers. It is a national language of Benin, where French is the official language. Search results gave dictionaries and reference works but no school textbook series.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No school-level Fon textbook series was found. Lexilogos mentions the Institut p\'edagogique d'\'etude et de d\'eveloppement de l'\'ecrit en fongb\'e, which deals with writing the language and is a lead for classroom material.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Wikipedia, Fon language (overview; sources differ on the phoneme inventory): \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fon_language}
\item H\"oftmann, \emph{Dictionnaire Fon--Fran\c{c}ais} (2003, about 8,000 entries, grammar sketch, standard orthography, five tone levels; Koeppe): \url{https://www.koeppe.de/titel_print_dictionnaire-fon-francais}
\item \emph{Dictionnaire Fran\c{c}ais--Fon} (2012, 854 pages in two volumes; Koeppe): \url{https://koeppe.de/titel_dictionnaire-francais-fon}
\item Lexilogos, Fon dictionary and resources page (lists Poirot's \emph{La langue fongbe du B\'enin}, a Peace Corps learning guide, and a Fon keyboard): \url{https://www.lexilogos.com/fon_dictionnaire.htm}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
No religious or community-organization resources were identified in the search results.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
The Lexilogos page above offers a Fon keyboard for the special characters. No audio or video corpus was found in these searches.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Fon is a national language in Benin and is taught as a subject. The resources found are mostly in French, so a K-12 curriculum would need new teaching materials. The Institut p\'edagogique and Poirot's works should be examined first.
