\section{Introduction}
Siraya (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fos}) is a dormant Austronesian language of southwestern Taiwan. It is known from 17th-century Dutch missionary texts, and revival activity draws on them. Search results found scholarly works but no specific modern textbook or dictionary.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
Searches indicate that activists have produced textbooks and songbooks from the Dutch texts, but no specific title or URL was found. A 2008 glossary by the Tainan Pe-po Siraya Culture Association is mentioned on Wikipedia, with no details verified.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
\begin{itemize}
\item Joby, \emph{Language revitalisation using historical texts: The case of Siraya} (University of East Anglia; compares revived forms with the 17th-century record): \url{https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/99055}
\end{itemize}
Any heritage curriculum would rest on the historical texts and Adelaar's reconstruction, and should be developed with the Siraya community and its cultural associations.
