\section{Introduction}
Yerakai (also spelled Yerekai) is spoken in Yerakai ward, Ambunti Rural LLG, northern Papua New Guinea. Wikipedia and Ethnologue place it in the Sepik family. Glottolog finds no significant lexical relationship to other Sepik languages and classifies it as an isolate. Wikipedia reports 380 speakers (2000 census). Joshua Project reports 700 for the people group, which is a different measure. Glottolog gives its vitality as ``shifting''.
