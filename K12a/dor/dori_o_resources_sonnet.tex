\section{Introduction}
Dori'o (ISO 639-3 \texttt{dor}; also Kwarekwareo) is an Oceanic language of South Malaita, Solomon Islands, with about 2,400 speakers (1999 figure). Searches found only general reference pages and no educational materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Dori'o were found in search results.
