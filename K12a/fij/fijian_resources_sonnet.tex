\section{Introduction}
Fijian (Bauan standard; ISO 639-3 fij) is the indigenous language of Fiji. This dossier lists only resources that appeared in web search results; coverage is thin, and no modern complete school textbook series was found.
\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Fiji government report that the Ministry's Curriculum Development Unit has written vernacular syllabuses, and that conversational Vosa Vakaviti is taught: \url{https://www.fiji.gov.fj/Media-Centre/News/Education-Minister-clarifies-vernaculars-taught-at-school}
\item Ministerial meeting with senior education specialists for languages: \url{https://www.fiji.gov.fj/Media-Centre/News/MINISTER-KUMAR-MEETS-WITH-SENIOR-EDUCATION-SPECIAL}
\end{itemize}
\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Sch\"utz, \emph{Spoken Fijian} (1971), an intensive course in Bauan Fijian with grammatical notes and glossary, listed as open access: \url{https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/10b85dda-09ab-45e4-8be8-d1707f1433cb}
\item Historical Fijian--English and English--Fijian dictionary (1872) catalogued by the National Library of Ireland: \url{https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000416860}
\end{itemize}

None were found in the searches performed.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Reports indicate Vosa Vakaviti is taught mainly as a conversational, optional subject, and that a curriculum review involving culture and heritage was reported in 2026: \url{https://www.ifacca.org/news/2026/03/11/culture-and-heritage-be-reflected-curriculum/}. A modern standard dictionary and full graded readers were not found.
