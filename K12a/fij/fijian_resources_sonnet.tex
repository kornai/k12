\section{Introduction}
Fijian (fij; Austronesian) is an indigenous language of Fiji. Standard Fijian is based on the Bau dialect, which missionaries used for Bible translation. This report is based on a small number of web searches and is deliberately short; no Fijian-medium school textbooks or Ministry of Education curriculum documents were found in the search results.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers or textbooks for K-12 use were found in the search results. A university library page on Fijian Language Week describes Bauan (Standard Fijian) and mentions library holdings of Fijian dictionaries, phrase books and recordings:
\begin{itemize}
\item Fijian Language Week 2020, University of Auckland Library: \url{https://www.news.library.auckland.ac.nz/2020/10/01/macawa-ni-vosa-vakaviti-fijian-language-week-2020/}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item R.M.W. Dixon, \emph{A Grammar of Boumaa Fijian} (University of Chicago Press, 1988), a scholarly grammar of the Boumaa dialect, not of Bauan; catalogue listing: \url{https://lehmanns.ch/shop/geisteswissenschaften/431329-9780864422194-fijian}
\item Capell, \emph{A New Fijian Dictionary} (1941), as a source record in the Tonga/Polynesian lexical database: \url{https://tlopo.clld.org/sources/Capell1941}
\item Austronesian Basic Vocabulary Database, Fijian (Suva dialect) word list: \url{https://abvd.eva.mpg.de/austronesian/language.php?id=653}
\item Swarthmore linguistics wiki page on Fijian (computational resources and text sources): \url{https://wikis.swarthmore.edu/ling073/Fijian}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
The search results mention the Bible translation in Bauan only indirectly (via the sources above); no specific scripture URL was found.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
Only the pages listed above were found; they mention recordings and online tools but no directly usable audio URL was seen.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Little verifiable curriculum material was found. A Fijian-medium K-12 curriculum would need to start from the reference works above and from direct contact with Fiji's Ministry of Education, which the searches did not reach.
