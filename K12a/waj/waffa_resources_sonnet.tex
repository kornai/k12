\section{Introduction}
Waffa (ISO 639-3 waj; Glottocode waff1241) is a Kainantu-family language of Papua New Guinea. It is spoken in villages at the headwaters of the Waffa River, a tributary of the Markham, in the Kainantu and Kaiapit districts. Sources give about 1,200 speakers (1979) and 1,990 (2000), and mention a central dialect and a small Urint dialect. Little published material turned up in search.
