\section{Introduction}
Turka (ISO 639-3 tuz; Glottocode turk1306; also called Tchourama) is a Gur language of southwest Burkina Faso with roughly 37,000 to 40,000 speakers. It is closest to Cerma, but the two are not mutually intelligible. Search results show a bilingual lexicon, a phonology sketch and a Webonary site, but no school materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
None were found.

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item Suggett and Suggett (2003), a French--Turka and Turka--French lexicon of about 2500 words, listed by Glottolog: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/sil16:44699}
\item Webonary site for Turka (Tchourama), which hosts the dictionary and a grammar section: \url{https://www.webonary.work/tchourama/language/grammar/?lang=en}
\item Colin Suggett, Turka Phonology Sketch (2005), covering sounds, phonotactics and an orthography proposal: \url{https://www.webonary.work/tchourama/files/Turka_Phonology_Sketch.pdf}
\item Omniglot page on Turka writing: \url{https://omniglot.com/writing/turka.htm}
\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
A dictionary and an orthography proposal exist, which gives a base for developing materials. No primers, textbooks or school programmes were found. The Latin script is used, with nasalization marked by a tilde and tone by diacritics. This dossier is short because little was found.
