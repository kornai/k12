\section{Introduction}
Moksela (ISO 639-3 vms, Glottocode moks1249) is an extinct and unattested language of Indonesia. Wikipedia places it on Buru Island, North Maluku, while a redirected version places it in the Sula Islands. Its classification is presumed, not demonstrated: probably Malayo-Polynesian (Central Maluku), based on its location. Wikipedia gives an extinction date of 1974. An Antara/Tempo report and a 2024 Education Ministry announcement list it among Indonesia's extinct languages. No texts, wordlists or recordings were found. A language curriculum is therefore not possible. Only a heritage-awareness component about the Buru and Sula region can be built.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Because nothing of the language is attested, revitalization is not feasible. Schools in the region could teach about the language's history and the neighbouring living languages such as Sula, using the sources above. The Tempo report below is background on extinct languages in Indonesia.
