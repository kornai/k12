\section{Introduction}
Ujir (ISO 639-3 udj) is an Austronesian language of the northwestern Aru Islands, Maluku, Indonesia. It is spoken in the villages of Ujir and Samang by about 1,000 people (2011). Sources say children no longer acquire it and that one of the two villages has stopped using it. Malay is displacing it.
