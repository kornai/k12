\section{Introduction}
Gagadu (also Gaagudju, Gagudju, Kakadu; ISO 639-3 gbu; Glottocode gaga1251; AIATSIS N50) is an extinct Aboriginal language of the Kakadu region of the Northern Territory, Australia. Its last fluent speaker, Big Bill Neidjie, died in 2002. Its family affiliation is disputed. My searches found only general reference entries and no teaching materials.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Gagadu were found in search results.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Very little is publicly documented in what I found. Any heritage curriculum would need to start from archival material (for example via AIATSIS) and work with the Traditional Owners of the Kakadu region and the groups who used Gagadu as a second language.
