\section{Introduction}
Even (ISO 639-3 \texttt{eve}; Glottocode even1260) is a Tungusic language of the Russian Federation, severely endangered according to the sources found. This report is based on a single round of web searches; little current school material was found, and the list below is short on purpose.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
\begin{itemize}
\item Library record for a reader for the 3rd class of the Even (Lamut) primary school by N.~P. Tkachik (Leningrad, 1948, 208 pages): \url{https://www.spri.cam.ac.uk/library/catalogue/records/21450/}
\item Historical overview of Even literacy (1858 primer and prayer book, 1930 handwritten primer, textbooks from the 1920s to 1990s): \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_alphabets}
\item Wikipedia note that the 1858 primer was in the Ola dialect of Even: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evenki_orthography}
\end{itemize}

\section{Linguistic and Reference Materials}
\begin{itemize}
\item General description of the language: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_language}
\item Endangered Languages Project entry: \url{https://www.endangeredlanguages.com/lang/1206/guide/6032}
\item Girfanova, Tungus-Manchu languages in Russia (sociolinguistic situation): \url{https://www.altaist.org/home/confessions/girfanova-albina-h/tungus-manchu-languages-in-russia-sociolinguistical-situation/}
\item Tarasova, publication on Even (PDF): \url{https://dh-north.org/siberian_studies/publications/betarasova.pdf}
\end{itemize}

\section{Religious and Community Resources}
The searches mention a missionary primer and prayer book (1858), a dictionary (1859) and a Gospel of Matthew (1880) in the Ola dialect, but no accessible copy was found; see \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_alphabets}. No community organisation was identified.

\section{Digital and Audio Resources}
No audio, video, corpus or software resources were found in these searches.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The only school materials found are historical (1930s--1990s). No current Even textbooks or dictionaries were located, so any curriculum would need to start from archival material and direct contact with Even communities and Russian regional education bodies, which this search did not identify.
