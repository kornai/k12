\section{Introduction}
Dass (ISO 639-3 \texttt{dot}; Glottocode dass1243) is an Afro-Asiatic (West Chadic) language of Bauchi State, Nigeria, also called Barawa. Search results list dialects including Durr-Baraza, Zumbul, Wandi and Dot, and cite about 8,800 speakers from a 1971 census figure. Very little educational material was found; this report is deliberately short.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}
No primers, textbooks or school programmes for Dass were found in the search results.

\item Ronald Cosper (1999), \emph{Barawa Lexicon}, a wordlist of South Bauchi languages including Dott (mapped by Glottolog to Dass); the Glottolog entry notes a critical response by B.\ Caron: \url{https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/136865}

\end{itemize}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
The language is not known to be taught in schools, and no orthography standard, curriculum or community organisation was identified in the search results. A native-language curriculum would need to start from primary documentation (the Cosper and Caron works) and new community-led work. Variety differences (Durr-Baraza, Zumbul, Dot, Wandi) would have to be settled first.
