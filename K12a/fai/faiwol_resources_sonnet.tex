\section{Introduction}
Faiwol (ISO 639-3: fai) is a Mountain Ok language of the Trans-New Guinea family, spoken in the Telefomin District (Sandaun Province) and the Tabubil area (Western Province) of Papua New Guinea, with some speakers across the border in Indonesia. Estimates run from about 3,200 to 4,500 speakers. It should not be confused with the neighbouring Telefol (tlf). The searches found no school materials, dictionary or grammar for Faiwol; what exists online is mostly a writing-system note, a numeral-system description and Christian audio programmes.

\section{Primary Educational Resources}

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Very little published material exists. A curriculum would need new work: collecting texts and orals, documenting the Latin-based orthography in use, and building on the base-27 counting system for mathematics. Neighbouring Telefol materials should not be assumed to apply to Faiwol.
