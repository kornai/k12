\section{Introduction}
Faiwol (ISO 639-3 \texttt{fai}) is an Ok language of the Trans-New Guinea family, spoken at the headwaters of the Fly, Palmer and Murray rivers in Western Province, Papua New Guinea, with some speakers across the border in Indonesia. Sources disagree on speaker numbers (about 4,500 in older figures, about 3,200 in a 2010 record). Faiwol is distinct from the neighbouring Telefol language. This search found only reference-level material and no teaching materials.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
Very little published material exists for Faiwol. Any native-language curriculum would need new primary documentation, orthography work and community partnership. Location and speaker figures should be verified against primary sources before use.
