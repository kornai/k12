\section{Introduction}
Zula (ISO 639-3 zla; Glottocode zula1235; also Shuula, Kishuula, Ba-Shuula) is a Bantu language of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Joshua Project reports about 33,000 Zula people in the DRC. It lists the language as their primary language, as unwritten, and as having Bible translation begun but no audio Bible. Zula is close to Enya, which has a different ISO code (gey), and the two should not be confused. Very little material was found, and nothing educational.

\section{Curriculum Development Context}
There is no written standard, so a curriculum would need to begin with orthography development, community consultation and fieldwork. Sources found are sparse and external; no Zula-led organizations were identified. Regional languages of education in the DRC are likely to be the practical medium until Zula materials exist, but this was not verified.
